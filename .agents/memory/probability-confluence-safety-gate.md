---
name: Probability confluence safety gate
description: Shared deterministic signal rules for first-page analysis and automated momentum entries.
---

All directional UI signals and automatic momentum entries must consume the same server-side technical-confluence snapshot. A bullish trend is not a BUY: long entry also requires acceptable location and risk/reward. Nearby resistance, an upper-range location, an unconfirmed break, or a failed break must yield WAIT even when structure is bullish. Automated long entry is allowed only with fresh, complete multi-timeframe evidence and a critical invalidation level. Missing or stale evidence must yield HOLD/NO TRADE, not a client-side directional fallback.

**Why:** Trend strength previously inflated BUY confidence while price was directly beneath resistance, confusing bullish bias with current entry quality. A simplified search-page SELL also conflicted with a support bounce.

**How to apply:** When adding or changing a trading signal, preserve the shared deterministic source. Require completed-close, volume, and hold evidence before confirming a breakout. Narrative/AI output may explain it but must never raise its stage, invent an entry, or override a no-trade decision.
