---
name: Search Fibonacci confluence
description: Safety and methodology rules for Fibonacci evidence in stock Search.
---

Search Fibonacci analysis must use a confirmed swing built from completed daily sessions. Treat retracements and extensions as a deterministic price map, not a prediction.

**Why:** Fibonacci levels can add useful confluence near support, resistance, and continuation zones, but they do not demonstrate buyer or seller participation by themselves.

**How to apply:** Keep Fibonacci influence bounded and Search-only. It may adjust directional evidence and confidence, but it must never bypass freshness, structure, breakout, or volume-confirmation gates and must not alter automated bot execution.
