---
name: Search signal isolation
description: Durable boundary and fail-safe rules for search-page volume analysis.
---

Search-page volume and price signals are read-only analysis. They must remain separate from bot confluence contracts, schedulers, entry gates, broker operations, and strategy settings. Search verdicts are binary BUY/SELL. Missing or stale evidence keeps the verdict provisional, caps confidence at 35%, and states the data gap instead of implying confirmation. Every visible Search panel must consume one timestamped server snapshot; generative narrative and unsupported default probabilities must not supplement the verdict.

Support and resistance are conditional thresholds, never automatic order instructions. Display direction as BULLISH/BEARISH. Search presents one key bias level plus one nearest resistance above and one nearest support below. A break-and-hold above/below the bias level establishes bullish/bearish bias; nearby S/R are context, not triggers. Search owns the only visible verdict; realtime analysis belongs inside the same Stock Intelligence module as supporting intraday context, without its own ticker control, refresh action, or competing verdict. Broad-market direction, raw volume-flow labels, and uncorroborated headlines must not appear as alternate stock verdicts.

**Why:** Binary presentation must not hide weak evidence, parallel client fetches can make panels disagree, and news or generative prose must not substitute for mechanical price and volume confirmation. Separate Search, Game Plan, and Realtime Trader cards made one stock appear to have multiple conclusions. The user approved the simplified single-verdict presentation for real-world accuracy testing.

**How to apply:** Keep bot-facing outputs unchanged. Share the authoritative Search response and refresh promise across header, signal, intraday context, levels, and news. Keep unique intraday evidence such as VWAP, volume profile, pivots, and patterns, but never add a second verdict. Search Key Bias and daily pivots must anchor to the latest completed regular session; 14-session S/R uses only completed bars and is withheld until a genuine ATR(14) buffer is available. Keep the Search display simple: one exact bias price, one exact immediate resistance, and one exact immediate support—never show low/high zone endpoints. Apply fresh entity-linked news only when price and volume corroborate it; ignore stale, future-dated, or unassociated headlines.
