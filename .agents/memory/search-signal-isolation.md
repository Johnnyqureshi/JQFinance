---
name: Search signal isolation
description: Durable boundary and fail-safe rules for search-page volume analysis.
---

Search-page volume and price signals are read-only analysis. They must remain separate from bot confluence contracts, schedulers, entry gates, broker operations, and strategy settings. The prominent verdict must remain visible at the top of the stock result, before the research tabs and detailed evidence. BUY and SELL require complete fresh volume evidence plus matching confirmed swing structure. Missing, stale, mixed, insufficient, or conflicting evidence must show WAIT with neutral direction and zero directional confidence; absence of bullish proof is never bearish evidence. Every visible Search panel must consume one timestamped server snapshot; generative narrative and unsupported default probabilities must not supplement the verdict.

Bullish Search structure requires confirmed swing evidence: HH+HL, or a lower-high transition whose break is held by completed closes. LH+HL is mixed, not bullish; higher lows, momentum, volume, indicators, news, and short-term price gains cannot override an intact lower high. Insufficient pivots fail closed for bullish classification, and mixed or conflicting structure materially reduces confidence.

Support and resistance are conditional thresholds, never automatic order instructions. Display direction as BULLISH/BEARISH. Search presents one key bias level plus one nearest resistance above and one nearest support below. A break-and-hold above/below the bias level establishes bullish/bearish bias; nearby S/R are context, not triggers. Search owns the only visible verdict; realtime analysis belongs inside the same Stock Intelligence module as supporting intraday context, without its own ticker control, refresh action, or competing verdict. Broad-market direction, raw volume-flow labels, and uncorroborated headlines must not appear as alternate stock verdicts.

The top Search trade plan may consolidate one Buy and one Sell reference from the strongest nearby cluster of real deterministic levels. Completed-session support/resistance remains the primary price anchor; fresh VWAP, Smart Money zones, and confirmed-swing Fibonacci may add bounded alignment. Trend, momentum, volume, price action, market regime, catalysts, and freshness control readiness/status but must never manufacture or shift a price without a real level. Only complete, fresh, entry-eligible, confirmed evidence may label the pair VERIFIED; otherwise show PROVISIONAL or WAITING. These references remain Search-only and never feed bot execution.

**Why:** The top Search answer is the primary outcome and must not disappear when duplicate analysis modules are consolidated. A default SELL once appeared during a sharp MSTR rally solely because bullish structure and acceleration were unavailable; binary presentation hid the lack of evidence. Parallel client fetches can make panels disagree, and news or generative prose must not substitute for mechanical price and volume confirmation. Separate Search, Game Plan, and Realtime Trader cards made one stock appear to have multiple conclusions. Range-based “higher lows” once allowed a bullish verdict while confirmed lower highs remained intact.

**How to apply:** Keep bot-facing outputs unchanged. Share the authoritative Search response and refresh promise across header, signal, intraday context, levels, news, and the top trade plan. Keep unique intraday evidence such as VWAP, volume profile, pivots, and patterns, but never add a second verdict. Classify the latest two confirmed swing highs and lows explicitly, expose the break level and confirmation state for debugging, and require completed-close hold above an LH before treating mixed structure as bullish. Search Key Bias and daily pivots must anchor to the latest completed regular session; 14-session S/R uses only completed bars and is withheld until a genuine ATR(14) buffer is available. Keep the Search display simple: one exact bias price, one exact immediate resistance, and one exact immediate support—never show low/high zone endpoints. Apply fresh entity-linked news only when price and volume corroborate it; ignore stale, future-dated, or unassociated headlines.

Crypto Search uses a separate 24/7 evidence contract: one canonical set of completed Yahoo 5-minute bars supplies current price, rolling-24H change/range/VWAP, UTC-day RVOL, structure, and acceleration. Do not apply equity-session filtering, SPY regime, equity analogs, or separately timed quote fields to a crypto verdict.

**Why:** Mixing a crypto quote baseline with New York stock-session volume produced a positive 24-hour move alongside an apparently bearish “time-of-day” volume signal.

**How to apply:** Label crypto windows explicitly as 24H or UTC-day, expose chart-bar provenance, fail closed when the canonical series is unavailable, and key user-scoped Search caches by user, quote scope, and symbol.
