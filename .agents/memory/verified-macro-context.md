---
name: Verified macro context boundary
description: Why the sourced macro risk view is separate from trading decisions and geopolitical claims
---
Keep the verified macro view separate from seasonal heuristics, Search confidence, and all automated execution decisions. The measured score is a deterministic research heuristic, not a probability or validated trade filter. Any future execution use requires explicit paper validation first.

**Why:** Existing market prices and calendar heuristics are not authoritative evidence of Fed policy, Treasury observations, or geopolitical severity. Missing evidence must not become low-risk points or invented facts.

**How to apply:** Preserve observation timestamps separately from retrieval timestamps. Suppress the composite when core measured inputs are missing or stale; do not rescale the remaining weights. Label geopolitical coverage as unmeasured until a verified provider and defensible severity methodology exist, even when the measured macro score is available.
