20/50 Moving Average Crossover Trading Strategy

Build a fully automated stock-scanning and trading strategy based on the 20-day moving average (20MA) and 50-day moving average (50MA).

Core Trading Signal

Use the 20-day moving average as the FAST moving average and the 50-day moving average as the SLOW moving average.

BUY / LONG SIGNAL

Generate a BUY / LONG signal only when:

* The 20MA crosses ABOVE the 50MA.
* This must be an actual crossover event, meaning the previous relationship was:
    * Previous 20MA <= Previous 50MA
    * Current 20MA > Current 50MA
* Do NOT generate repeated buy signals simply because the 20MA remains above the 50MA.
* Once a position is entered, do not open another position from the same crossover unless the position has been completely exited and a new valid crossover occurs.

EXIT SIGNAL

Generate an EXIT / SELL signal when:

* The 20MA crosses BELOW the 50MA.
* This must be an actual crossover event:
    * Previous 20MA >= Previous 50MA
    * Current 20MA < Current 50MA

When this occurs, immediately close the associated long position.

MARKET / STOCK FILTERS

Do NOT trade every crossover.

Only consider stocks that satisfy ALL of the following conditions:

1. Price: $1.00–$20.00
2. Relative Volume: >= 2.0x normal volume
3. Daily Performance: Stock is already up at least +10% on the trading day
4. Trend: The stock must demonstrate a clear directional trend.
5. Volume: High volume must support the move.
6. Liquidity: Avoid securities with extremely poor liquidity, extremely wide bid/ask spreads, or insufficient trading volume to execute reliably.
7. Choppy Market Filter: Do NOT enter trades when price action is sideways, whipsawing, or repeatedly crossing the 20MA and 50MA without establishing a directional trend.

TREND QUALITY FILTER

The bot must distinguish a genuine trend from a choppy environment.

Prefer stocks where:

* 20MA is beginning to slope upward after the bullish crossover.
* 50MA is flat-to-rising rather than sharply declining.
* Price is trading above the 20MA and 50MA.
* Volume is substantially elevated.
* Price action is making higher highs and higher lows.
* The crossover is supported by strong price momentum rather than occurring during sideways consolidation.

Reject setups where:

* 20MA and 50MA repeatedly cross back and forth.
* Price is oscillating around both moving averages.
* Volume is weak.
* The stock is trading sideways.
* The crossover occurs without meaningful price expansion.
* The stock has an extremely wide bid/ask spread or insufficient liquidity.

STOCK UNIVERSE

The scanner must scan the ENTIRE universe of tradable securities available through Alpaca, rather than scanning only a predefined watchlist or a small list of popular stocks.

The universe must include:

* All eligible U.S. stocks available through Alpaca.
* ETFs.
* Leveraged ETFs.
* Inverse leveraged ETFs.
* Other eligible exchange-traded securities supported by the Alpaca account/API.

Do NOT hard-code a small ticker list.

Refresh the Alpaca asset universe so newly available securities can be considered.

Before submitting an order, verify that the security is:

* Tradable.
* Active.
* Not halted.
* Supported by the account.
* Has sufficient real-time market data.
* Meets all strategy filters.

SETUP PRIORITIZATION

If multiple securities produce valid signals simultaneously, rank candidates by:

1. Relative volume
2. Daily percentage gain
3. Trend strength
4. Volume confirmation
5. Quality of the 20MA/50MA crossover
6. Liquidity / execution quality

Do not enter every qualifying stock indiscriminately if doing so would exceed the portfolio’s risk limits.

STOP LOSS

Every newly opened position must have a 5% stop loss.

Initial stop:

Stop Price = Entry Price × 0.95

The stop must be established immediately after entering the position.

The 5% stop loss is independent of the 20MA/50MA exit signal.

Therefore, exit the position when either:

* The 5% stop loss is triggered, OR
* The 20MA crosses below the 50MA.

POSITION MANAGEMENT

Never enter a position without a defined stop loss.

Never remove the 5% protective stop simply because the stock continues moving against the position.

After a stop-loss exit, do not immediately re-enter unless a new valid 20MA/50MA bullish crossover event occurs and all filters are satisfied again.

IMPORTANT IMPLEMENTATION REQUIREMENTS

The crossover must be calculated from historical price data rather than simply checking whether:

20MA > 50MA

The system must specifically detect the transition from:

20MA <= 50MA

to:

20MA > 50MA

for a bullish crossover.

Likewise, the bearish exit must detect the transition from:

20MA >= 50MA

to:

20MA < 50MA

Prevent duplicate orders, duplicate entries, and repeated signals caused by multiple scanner cycles.

The strategy should be designed to run continuously during market hours and safely handle:

* Market open
* Trading halts
* Missing market data
* API errors
* Order rejection
* Partial fills
* Position already exists
* Duplicate signals
* Market close
* Alpaca connection/API interruptions

PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

The strategy is designed to capture strongly trending, high-volume momentum moves following a confirmed 20MA/50MA bullish crossover, while avoiding sideways/choppy securities.

The bot should favor quality setups over quantity.

A valid crossover alone is NOT sufficient.

The complete entry requirement is:

20MA bullish crossover + 2x+ relative volume + 10%+ daily gain + $1-$20 price + strong trend + non-choppy price action + adequate liquidity.

Execute a long trade only when the complete setup is confirmed.
