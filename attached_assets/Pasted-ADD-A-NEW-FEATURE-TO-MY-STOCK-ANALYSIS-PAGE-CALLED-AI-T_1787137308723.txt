ADD A NEW FEATURE TO MY STOCK ANALYSIS PAGE CALLED:

"AI TRADE GAME PLAN"

The purpose of this feature is to analyze a stock's CURRENT market setup and produce a probability-based intraday trading game plan.

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT make simplistic conclusions such as:
"RSI is oversold = BUY."

The AI must analyze multiple factors together and explain what is most likely to happen, what would invalidate the setup, and what price levels traders should watch.

==================================================
1. REAL-TIME DATA
==================================================

Use the best available real-time market data source already connected to this application.

For the selected ticker, retrieve:

- Current price
- Previous close
- Today's open
- Today's high
- Today's low
- Premarket price
- Premarket high/low
- Volume
- Average volume
- Relative volume
- Market capitalization
- 52-week high/low
- ATR
- RSI (14)
- VWAP
- EMA 9
- EMA 20
- SMA 50
- SMA 200
- MACD
- Recent daily price history
- Recent intraday price history when available

If real-time data is unavailable, clearly display:

"REAL-TIME DATA UNAVAILABLE"

Do NOT fabricate missing data.

==================================================
2. CALCULATE THE RECENT SELL-OFF
==================================================

Calculate:

- Previous day's percentage change
- 2-day percentage change
- 5-day percentage change
- 10-day percentage change
- Distance from recent high
- Distance from 20-day moving average
- Distance from 50-day moving average
- ATR as a percentage of price
- Current volume versus average volume

Identify whether the stock experienced:

- Normal decline
- Sharp decline
- Extreme decline
- Gap-down
- Gap-up
- High-volume liquidation
- Low-volume pullback

Example:

"Stock declined 9.7% yesterday, which is an unusually large one-day move relative to its recent ATR."

==================================================
3. OVERSOLD ANALYSIS
==================================================

Analyze RSI but NEVER use RSI by itself.

Evaluate:

- RSI 14
- RSI 5
- RSI trend
- RSI divergence if sufficient data exists
- Distance from VWAP
- Distance from short-term moving averages
- Recent consecutive red/green candles
- Size of recent candles
- Volume expansion
- Whether the stock is extended below its normal trading range

Classify the setup:

VERY OVERSOLD
OVERSOLD
NEUTRAL
OVERBOUGHT
VERY OVERBOUGHT

Explain WHY.

IMPORTANT:
An oversold condition does NOT automatically mean the stock will rise.

==================================================
4. HISTORICAL ANALOG ANALYSIS
==================================================

When enough historical data exists, search the stock's historical price data for similar setups.

Look for previous occasions where:

- The stock dropped approximately 5%+
- The stock dropped approximately 8%+
- The stock dropped approximately 10%+
- RSI reached similar levels
- Volume was unusually high
- The stock was similarly extended from VWAP/moving averages

For comparable historical setups, calculate:

- Percentage of times the stock bounced intraday
- Average maximum intraday rebound
- Median maximum intraday rebound
- Percentage that closed green
- Average next-day return
- Maximum downside after the comparable setup

Do NOT claim statistical certainty if the sample size is small.

Show:

"HISTORICAL SAMPLE SIZE: X"

If fewer than 10 meaningful historical examples exist, explicitly state:

"Limited historical sample — probability confidence is LOW."

==================================================
5. MARKET + SECTOR CONTEXT
==================================================

Analyze the broader market.

For U.S. stocks, evaluate when possible:

- SPY
- QQQ
- IWM
- Relevant sector ETF
- Major industry peers

Determine whether the stock's move appears to be:

1. Stock-specific
2. Sector-wide
3. Broad market driven
4. Combination

Example:

"SNDK declined 9%, while the semiconductor sector declined 4%. This suggests both sector pressure and additional stock-specific selling."

Do NOT assume correlation without actual data.

==================================================
6. NEWS / CATALYST ANALYSIS
==================================================

Retrieve recent relevant news when available.

Look for:

- Earnings
- Guidance
- Analyst upgrades/downgrades
- Price target changes
- SEC filings
- Insider activity
- Product announcements
- M&A
- Government/regulatory developments
- Sector news
- Company-specific negative catalysts
- Company-specific positive catalysts

Determine whether the selloff appears fundamentally driven or primarily technical.

If no significant catalyst is found, say:

"No major catalyst identified from available data."

==================================================
7. INTRADAY BOUNCE PROBABILITY
==================================================

Estimate probabilities for:

A. Probability of an intraday relief bounce
B. Probability of closing green
C. Probability of a 3%+ rebound from the day's low
D. Probability of a 5%+ rebound from the day's low
E. Probability of another major downside leg

IMPORTANT:

These are estimates, NOT guarantees.

The model must combine:

- Technical indicators
- Magnitude of selloff
- Historical analogs
- Volume
- Volatility
- Premarket behavior
- Support/resistance
- Market direction
- Sector direction
- News/catalysts

Do NOT create fake precision.

For example:

"Estimated probability of an intraday relief bounce: 65%"

is acceptable.

But explain the reasoning behind the number.

==================================================
8. SUPPORT AND RESISTANCE
==================================================

Automatically identify important levels.

Display:

KEY SUPPORT
SECONDARY SUPPORT
BREAKDOWN LEVEL

CURRENT PRICE

FIRST RESISTANCE
SECONDARY RESISTANCE
MAJOR RESISTANCE

Use:

- Previous day's high/low
- Premarket high/low
- VWAP
- Recent swing highs/lows
- Moving averages
- High-volume price levels when available
- ATR

Avoid generating arbitrary levels.

==================================================
9. OPENING GAME PLAN
==================================================

Generate three possible scenarios.

SCENARIO 1 — BULLISH REVERSAL

Explain:

- What needs to happen
- Entry confirmation
- Important support
- Confirmation level
- Potential targets
- What invalidates the setup

Example structure:

"Bullish if price holds $X and reclaims $Y with increasing volume."

SCENARIO 2 — RANGE / CHOP

Explain:

- Expected trading range
- Important levels
- Why traders may want to wait

SCENARIO 3 — BEARISH CONTINUATION

Explain:

- Breakdown level
- Confirmation criteria
- Potential downside levels
- What would invalidate the bearish scenario

==================================================
10. DO NOT CATCH A FALLING KNIFE
==================================================

The system must explicitly warn users against buying solely because the stock is down heavily.

For example:

"Do not assume the stock has bottomed simply because it is down 10%. Wait for evidence of stabilization or reversal."

Look for confirmation such as:

- Higher low
- VWAP reclaim
- Break of opening range high
- Increasing buying volume
- Reclaim of important support
- Bullish divergence
- Sector stabilization

==================================================
11. TRADE SETUP
==================================================

If the setup is favorable, generate:

IDEAL ENTRY ZONE
CONFIRMATION ENTRY
STOP / INVALIDATION LEVEL
TARGET 1
TARGET 2
TARGET 3
RISK/REWARD

However:

If there is no attractive setup, say:

"NO TRADE — WAIT FOR CONFIRMATION."

Never force a trade.

==================================================
12. CONFIDENCE SCORE
==================================================

Create a confidence score from 0–100 based on the quality of the evidence.

Break it into:

Technical Setup
Historical Evidence
Market Context
Sector Context
Catalyst Risk
Volume Confirmation
Risk/Reward

Example:

OVERALL SETUP CONFIDENCE: 74/100

Then explain the strongest reasons supporting the score and the biggest risks.

Update the analysis when:

- Price moves significantly
- VWAP changes
- Volume changes materially
- Support/resistance breaks
- Market direction changes
- Sector direction changes
- New significant news appears

Do not continuously regenerate expensive AI analysis every second.

Use sensible throttling/debouncing.

==================================================
16. IMPORTANT SAFETY / ACCURACY RULES
==================================================

The AI is an analytical decision-support tool, not a guarantee of future stock performance.

Never:

- Fabricate market data
- Fabricate news
- Fabricate historical statistics
- Claim certainty
- Say a stock MUST bounce
- Say oversold automatically means BUY
- Generate a trade solely from RSI
- Hide missing data

When data is missing, explicitly state that it is missing.

When historical sample size is small, lower confidence.

The system should prefer:

"Evidence currently favors a bounce"

instead of:

"The stock will bounce."

==================================================
17. AI RESPONSE STYLE
==================================================

The final analysis should be concise enough for a trader to read quickly.

Use this order:

1. AI VERDICT
2. PROBABILITIES
3. WHY
4. KEY LEVELS
5. BULLISH SCENARIO
6. BEARISH SCENARIO
7. WHAT WOULD CHANGE THE THESIS
8. RISK
9. FINAL GAME PLAN

The goal is to make the analysis feel like an experienced trader is looking at the entire setup rather than simply reading technical indicators.
