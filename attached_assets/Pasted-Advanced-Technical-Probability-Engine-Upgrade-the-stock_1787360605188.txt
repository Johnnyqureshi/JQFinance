Advanced Technical Probability Engine

Upgrade the stock analysis engine so it does NOT attempt to predict market movements with certainty.

The system must instead calculate the probability and strength of potential bullish continuation, bullish reversal/bounce, bearish continuation, and bearish reversal/drawdown setups using technical confluence.

PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

For every stock being analyzed, calculate:

* Bullish Probability Score: 0–100
* Bearish Probability Score: 0–100
* Bounce Probability: 0–100
* Drawdown Probability: 0–100
* Setup Quality: 0–100
* Trend State
* Momentum State
* Volume State
* Risk State

Never describe a prediction as guaranteed.

Use language such as:

* “High-probability bullish setup”
* “Moderate bullish setup”
* “Neutral / no clear edge”
* “Elevated drawdown risk”
* “High-probability bearish setup”

1. PRICE ACTION / MARKET STRUCTURE

Analyze:

* Higher highs
* Higher lows
* Lower highs
* Lower lows
* Breakouts
* Breakdown attempts
* Failed breakouts
* Failed breakdowns
* Consolidation
* Range expansion
* Range contraction
* Gap-ups
* Gap-downs

Determine:

TREND = BULLISH / BEARISH / SIDEWAYS

Identify the most important recent support and resistance levels.

Give greater weight to levels that have been tested multiple times.

2. VOLUME ANALYSIS

Calculate:

* Current volume
* Relative volume
* Average volume
* Volume spike percentage
* Volume trend
* Up-volume versus down-volume
* Volume confirmation of price movement

Detect:

* Breakout + volume confirmation
* Breakdown + volume confirmation
* Price increase without volume confirmation
* Price decrease without volume confirmation
* Selling exhaustion
* Buying exhaustion
* Volume climax
* Volume contraction before potential expansion

IMPORTANT:

A price breakout without meaningful volume confirmation should receive a lower confidence score.

A price breakout accompanied by significantly elevated relative volume should receive a higher confidence score.

3. RSI ANALYSIS

Calculate RSI using the configured timeframe.

Interpret RSI in context.

Do NOT automatically treat RSI <30 as a BUY signal.

Use:

RSI <30:
Potentially oversold.

RSI 30–40:
Potential recovery zone.

RSI 40–50:
Neutral/recovery momentum.

RSI 50–60:
Bullish momentum developing.

RSI 60–70:
Strong bullish momentum.

RSI 70–75:
Extended but potentially still trending.

RSI >=75:
Potential exhaustion / take-profit zone.

Detect RSI divergence:

* Bullish divergence
* Bearish divergence

Bullish divergence should increase bounce probability when price is near meaningful support.

Bearish divergence should increase drawdown probability when price is near meaningful resistance.

4. VWAP ANALYSIS

Calculate intraday VWAP.

Determine:

* Price above VWAP
* Price below VWAP
* VWAP slope
* VWAP reclaim
* VWAP rejection
* VWAP breakout
* VWAP breakdown

Bullish signals:

Price above VWAP + rising VWAP + increasing volume.

Potential reversal signal:

Price below VWAP → reclaims VWAP → holds VWAP → volume increases.

Bearish signals:

Price below VWAP + declining VWAP + increasing selling volume.

Potential bearish reversal:

Price above VWAP → loses VWAP → fails to reclaim it.

5. MOVING AVERAGES

Calculate at minimum:

* 9 EMA
* 20 EMA
* 50 SMA
* 200 SMA

Analyze:

* Price versus each moving average
* EMA crossovers
* Moving-average slope
* Distance from moving averages
* Reclaims
* Rejections

Short-term bullish confirmation:

9 EMA > 20 EMA with both rising.

Short-term bearish confirmation:

9 EMA < 20 EMA with both declining.

A large distance from the 9/20 EMA should be treated as an extension risk.

6. MACD

Calculate:

* MACD line
* Signal line
* Histogram

Detect:

* Bullish crossover
* Bearish crossover
* Increasing bullish histogram
* Decreasing bullish histogram
* Increasing bearish histogram
* Decreasing bearish histogram
* Bullish divergence
* Bearish divergence

Use MACD as confirmation rather than a standalone trading signal.

7. SUPPORT AND RESISTANCE ENGINE

Automatically identify:

* Recent swing highs
* Recent swing lows
* Previous day’s high
* Previous day’s low
* Premarket high
* Premarket low
* VWAP
* Major moving averages
* Recent consolidation ranges
* Breakout levels
* Breakdown levels
* Gap levels

Calculate distance from current price to each important level.

Detect:

SUPPORT TEST

RESISTANCE TEST

BREAKOUT

BREAKDOWN

FAILED BREAKOUT

FAILED BREAKDOWN

When price approaches resistance while RSI is elevated and bearish divergence exists, increase drawdown probability.

When price approaches support while RSI is oversold, selling volume is declining, and bullish divergence exists, increase bounce probability.

8. RELATIVE STRENGTH

Compare the stock against:

* SPY
* QQQ
* Relevant sector ETF

Calculate relative strength.

Examples:

Stock +5%, SPY +1% = strong relative strength.

Stock -3%, SPY -1% = weak relative strength.

A stock outperforming its sector and the broader market should receive additional bullish points.

A stock underperforming both should receive additional bearish points.

9. MARKET REGIME

Analyze the broader market before making a directional assessment.

Determine:

* SPY trend
* QQQ trend
* Market momentum
* Market volatility
* Sector trend
* Risk-on / risk-off environment

Do not evaluate a stock completely independently from the market.

10. CATALYST ANALYSIS

When reliable real-time data is available, analyze:

* Earnings
* Earnings surprises
* Guidance
* News
* FDA events
* Analyst upgrades
* Analyst downgrades
* SEC filings
* Offerings
* Dilution
* Insider activity
* Short interest
* Short squeeze potential
* Premarket gap
* Breaking news

Catalysts should modify the technical score rather than replace technical analysis.

11. BOUNCE DETECTION ENGINE

Create a dedicated BOUNCE SETUP score.

Increase bounce probability when multiple conditions occur simultaneously:

* RSI oversold
* Bullish RSI divergence
* Price at significant support
* Selling volume declining
* Volume exhaustion
* Price stops making new lows
* Bullish candle reversal
* MACD momentum improving
* Price reclaims 9 EMA
* Price reclaims VWAP
* Relative strength improves
* Market/sector stabilizes

Do NOT call a bounce simply because RSI is oversold.

Require multiple confirming signals.

12. DRAWDOWN DETECTION ENGINE

Create a dedicated DRAWDOWN RISK score.

Increase drawdown probability when multiple conditions occur simultaneously:

* Price loses major support
* High-volume selling
* Price below VWAP
* VWAP declining
* RSI weakening
* Bearish RSI divergence
* MACD bearish crossover
* 9 EMA crosses below 20 EMA
* Relative weakness versus SPY
* Relative weakness versus sector
* Failed breakout
* Bearish catalyst
* Breakdown confirmed by volume

13. BULLISH BREAKOUT DETECTION

A breakout should receive high confidence only when:

1. Price breaks meaningful resistance.
2. Volume increases significantly above average.
3. Price holds above the breakout level.
4. VWAP/short-term moving averages confirm direction.
5. RSI is strong but not excessively extended.
6. The broader market is not strongly bearish.

Detect and flag:

“BREAKOUT CONFIRMED”

or

“BREAKOUT NOT CONFIRMED”

14. FALSE BREAKOUT DETECTION

Detect:

* Price briefly moves above resistance
* Volume fails to confirm
* Price falls back below resistance
* RSI divergence
* Rejection candle
* VWAP loss

If detected:

“FALSE BREAKOUT / DRAWDOWN RISK”

15. SCORING SYSTEM

Create a weighted scoring model.

Example:

PRICE ACTION = 20%

VOLUME = 20%

VWAP = 15%

RSI = 10%

MACD = 10%

MOVING AVERAGES = 10%

SUPPORT/RESISTANCE = 10%

RELATIVE STRENGTH = 5%

MARKET REGIME = 5%

CATALYST = adjustment layer

Do not simply count indicators.

Signals should be weighted according to their importance and timeframe.

16. CONFLUENCE REQUIREMENT

The engine must distinguish between:

SINGLE SIGNAL

MULTIPLE CONFIRMATIONS

HIGH CONFLUENCE

Example:

RSI <30 alone:

“Bounce possible, but insufficient confirmation.”

RSI <30 + support + bullish divergence:

“Moderate bounce probability.”

RSI <30 + support + bullish divergence + declining selling volume + VWAP reclaim + MACD improvement:

“High-confluence bullish reversal setup.”

17. MULTI-TIMEFRAME ANALYSIS

Analyze at minimum:

* 1-minute
* 5-minute
* 15-minute
* 1-hour
* Daily

Use shorter timeframes for entry timing.

Use higher timeframes for trend direction.

Do not allow a 1-minute bullish signal to override a major daily bearish structure without explicitly explaining the conflict.

“RSI TAKE-PROFIT ZONE”

However, do not blindly exit solely because RSI reaches 75 if the user has configured a different strategy.

Allow RSI 75 to be configurable.

21. MOST IMPORTANT RULE

Never claim that the system knows a pump or drop “is coming.”

The system must identify probability and confluence.

The goal is:

“Find situations where the evidence is becoming increasingly asymmetric in one direction.”

Only generate a strong BUY/SELL signal when multiple independent technical factors agree.

If signals conflict, return:

“NO CLEAR EDGE”

instead of forcing a trade.

22. REAL-TIME DATA REQUIREMENT

Use the freshest available market data.

Clearly display the timestamp of the market data used.

If a required data source is delayed, unavailable, or stale, do not pretend it is real-time.

Flag:

“DATA STALE — SIGNAL CONFIDENCE REDUCED”

23. AUDIT TRAIL

For every generated signal, store the individual reasons behind the score.

Example:

Bullish factors:
+18 breakout volume
+12 VWAP reclaim
+10 bullish RSI divergence
+8 support confirmation
+6 relative strength

Bearish factors:
-7 elevated RSI
-5 resistance nearby

This allows the user to understand WHY the algorithm generated the signal instead of receiving an unexplained prediction.

FINAL PRINCIPLE

Build this as a probability/confluence engine, not a crystal ball.

The strongest setup is not “one indicator says BUY.”

The strongest setup is when:

PRICE ACTION + VOLUME + VWAP + MOMENTUM + SUPPORT/RESISTANCE + RELATIVE STRENGTH + MARKET REGIME

all point in the same direction.

When those signals disagree, reduce confidence or return NO CLEAR EDGE.
