PREMARKET SCANNER FEATURES TO CHECK AND IMPLEMENT:

1. PREMARKET GAINERS

* Find stocks with significant premarket percentage gains.
* Track current premarket price, previous close, and percentage change.
* Allow configurable minimum gain, such as +3%, +5%, +10%.

2. PREMARKET VOLUME

* Track premarket volume.
* Calculate relative volume when historical data is available.
* Identify unusual premarket volume.
* Rank stocks by volume and relative volume.

3. NEWS/CATALYST DETECTION
    Identify recent catalysts including:

* Earnings
* Earnings guidance
* FDA/biotech announcements
* Clinical trial results
* SEC filings
* Analyst upgrades/downgrades
* Price-target changes
* M&A
* Partnerships
* Contracts
* Government/regulatory announcements
* Product launches
* Major company announcements

Each stock should display the catalyst and source/time when available.

4. UNUSUAL OPTIONS ACTIVITY
    If the available market-data provider supports it:

* Detect unusual call/put volume.
* Detect unusual open interest.
* Detect large options trades.
* Calculate call/put activity where possible.

If the current API does not support options data, clearly mark this feature as unavailable rather than inventing data.

5. SHORT-SQUEEZE DATA
    When available, collect:

* Short interest
* Short float %
* Float
* Average volume
* Days to cover

Use this as a secondary scoring factor rather than automatically labeling something a squeeze.

6. TECHNICAL SETUPS
    Calculate:

* VWAP
* Premarket high
* Premarket low
* Previous day’s high
* Previous day’s low
* Previous close
* Support levels
* Resistance levels
* Breakout levels
* RSI

Detect setups such as:

* Premarket breakout
* Resistance breakout
* VWAP reclaim
* VWAP rejection
* Gap-and-go
* Gap-fill setup
* High-of-day breakout

7. SECTOR MOMENTUM

Examples:

* Semiconductors
* AI
* Biotech
* Energy
* Financials
* Crypto
* Silver/gold/mining
* EV
* Defense

Give additional confidence when the individual stock and its sector are moving together.

8. SENTIMENT
    When reliable news/social sentiment data is available:

* Calculate bullish/bearish sentiment.
* Do not rely exclusively on social-media sentiment.
* Show the underlying sources when possible.

9. STOCK QUALITY FILTER
    Allow configurable filters for:

* Market cap
* Share price
* Average volume
* Premarket volume
* Float
* Minimum liquidity

Prevent extremely illiquid stocks from automatically receiving high scores simply because they moved a lot.
