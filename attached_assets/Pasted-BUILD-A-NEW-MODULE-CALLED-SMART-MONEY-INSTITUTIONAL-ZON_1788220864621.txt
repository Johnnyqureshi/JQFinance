3. SWING HIGH / SWING LOW ENGINE
==================================================

Identify meaningful swing points algorithmically.

Use configurable pivot logic rather than simply identifying every local high/low.

Example:

Swing High:

High is greater than the highs of N candles on both sides.

Swing Low:

Low is lower than the lows of N candles on both sides.

Allow the lookback to adapt based on timeframe and ATR.

Classify swings:

Minor
Intermediate
Major

A major swing should require greater price displacement and/or structural importance.

Display important levels on the chart.

Example:

MAJOR SWING HIGH
$499.44

MAJOR SWING LOW
$215.47

==================================================
4. DEMAND / INSTITUTIONAL BUY ZONE ENGINE
==================================================

Identify potential demand zones by looking for areas from which substantial bullish displacement originated.

A candidate demand zone can be created when:

A) Price consolidates or bases

AND

B) Strong bullish displacement follows

AND

C) The displacement exceeds a configurable ATR threshold

AND/OR

D) Relative volume is elevated

AND/OR

E) Market structure shifts bullish

AND/OR

F) Price sweeps a previous low/liquidity level before reversing

AND/OR

G) An imbalance/FVG is created during the move

AND/OR

H) The area produces significant subsequent upside movement.

Possible zone construction:

For a bullish order-block style zone:

Zone High =
highest relevant candle/base high before displacement

Zone Low =
lowest relevant candle/base low before displacement

Do not automatically label every bullish candle an institutional zone.

Require confluence.

==================================================
5. SUPPLY / INSTITUTIONAL SELL ZONE ENGINE
==================================================

Mirror the demand-zone logic.

Identify areas from which substantial bearish displacement originated.

Conditions may include:

- Consolidation/base
- Strong bearish displacement
- Elevated relative volume
- Bearish market structure shift
- Liquidity sweep above a prior high
- Fair Value Gap
- Large ATR expansion
- Significant subsequent downside movement

Create a price range rather than a single line.

Label:

POTENTIAL SUPPLY ZONE

rather than claiming:

INSTITUTIONAL SELL ZONE

unless the confidence threshold is extremely high.

==================================================
7. LIQUIDITY SWEEPS
==================================================

Detect potential liquidity sweeps.

Bullish liquidity sweep:

Price trades below a significant prior swing low and then closes back above it.

Bearish liquidity sweep:

Price trades above a significant prior swing high and then closes back below it.

Record:

- Swept level
- Sweep candle
- Volume
- Reversal strength
- Subsequent displacement

Use this as a major confidence factor for zone detection.

==================================================
8. FAIR VALUE GAPS / IMBALANCES
==================================================

Detect three-candle imbalance structures.

Bullish FVG:

Current candle low > high of candle two candles earlier.

Bearish FVG:

Current candle high < low of candle two candles earlier.

Record:

- FVG high
- FVG low
- Size
- ATR-normalized size
- Whether filled
- Percentage filled
- Whether price reacted from it

FVGs should contribute to zone confidence.

Do not display every tiny FVG.

Filter by minimum size relative to ATR.

==================================================
9. DISPLACEMENT ENGINE
==================================================

Detect unusually strong directional moves.

Calculate:

Candle range / ATR

and

Move magnitude / ATR

Example:

If a bullish move is > 1.5 ATR from a base and has elevated volume, increase demand-zone confidence.

Create configurable thresholds.

==================================================
10. RELATIVE VOLUME
==================================================

Calculate:

Current Volume / Average Volume

Example:

RVOL = 2.4

means volume is approximately 2.4x the reference average.

Use relative volume as evidence, not proof of institutional activity.

Examples:

RVOL < 1:
little confirmation

RVOL 1–1.5:
normal

RVOL 1.5–2:
strong

RVOL > 2:
very strong

Make thresholds configurable.

==================================================
11. ZONE FRESHNESS
==================================================

Every zone must have a freshness score.

A newly created zone should receive a higher score.

Each subsequent test should be tracked.

Track:

- Creation date
- Number of touches
- First reaction
- Most recent reaction
- Whether zone was partially mitigated
- Whether zone was fully broken

Important:

Do not assume more touches = stronger.

Repeated tests can weaken a zone.

==================================================
12. ZONE INVALIDATION
==================================================

A zone must be marked invalid when price decisively breaks through it.

Define invalidation using:

- Candle close beyond zone
- ATR-adjusted penetration
- Volume confirmation

Avoid invalidating based on tiny intraday wicks.

Example:

Demand zone:

$300–$320

If price briefly trades to $298 but closes back above $300:

DO NOT automatically invalidate.

If price closes at $285 with strong volume:

ZONE INVALIDATED.
