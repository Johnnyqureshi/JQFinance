BUILD AN INTELLIGENT MULTI-WEEK / MULTI-MONTH SWING TRADING BOT

PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Upgrade the existing trading bot into an intelligent swing-trading system designed to:

1. Find fundamentally/technically high-quality stocks that have become significantly oversold.
2. Determine whether the selloff is temporary or represents genuine fundamental deterioration.
3. Identify high-probability support and reversal zones.
4. Enter positions only when multiple independent signals confirm the setup.
5. Hold positions for multiple weeks to multiple months when the trend remains healthy.
6. Automatically determine take-profit zones.
7. Scale out of positions intelligently rather than selling everything at one arbitrary target.
8. Protect capital with disciplined position sizing, stop-losses, trailing stops, and portfolio-level risk controls.
9. Continuously reassess every position as new price, volume, news, macroeconomic, and fundamental information becomes available.

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT simply buy stocks because RSI is low.

The system must distinguish between:

TEMPORARILY OVERSOLD
vs.
FUNDAMENTALLY BROKEN

The goal is to buy high-quality companies during significant but potentially temporary drawdowns.

8. 200 SMA BOUNCE DETECTION

⸻

Specifically detect stocks testing the 200 SMA.

Do NOT automatically buy a stock simply because it touches the 200 SMA.

Look for:

1. Price reaches 200 SMA.
2. Selling pressure begins decreasing.
3. Long lower wick/reversal candle appears.
4. Volume shows capitulation or stabilization.
5. Price stops making lower lows.
6. Higher low develops.
7. Price reclaims the reversal candle high.
8. Price reclaims VWAP/short-term moving averages when applicable.
9. RSI begins recovering from oversold conditions.

Classify:

200 SMA TEST
200 SMA DEFENSE
200 SMA CONFIRMED REVERSAL
200 SMA FAILURE

Only consider the strongest category for aggressive entry.
