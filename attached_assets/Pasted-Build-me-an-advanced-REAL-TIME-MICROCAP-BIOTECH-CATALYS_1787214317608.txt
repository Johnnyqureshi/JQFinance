Build me an advanced REAL-TIME MICROCAP & BIOTECH CATALYST TRADING INTELLIGENCE BOT.

The goal is to identify small-cap and micro-cap stocks BEFORE or immediately AFTER major news catalysts that could cause large price movements.

This is an intelligence/scanning system, NOT an autonomous trading system. Do NOT place trades automatically. The system should generate alerts, rankings, catalyst scores, risk assessments, and suggested price levels for human review.

==================================================

The scanner should continuously monitor new information.

Target latency:

As close to real-time as APIs permit.

Every new article, filing, announcement, or event should be:

1. Retrieved
2. Timestamped
3. Associated with a ticker
4. Associated with a company
5. Classified
6. Given a catalyst score
7. Given a confidence score
8. Compared against current stock price/volume
9. Sent through the alert engine

Avoid duplicate news.

If the same press release appears across multiple sources, merge it into one event.

==================================================

Classify catalysts into:

VERY BULLISH:

- FDA approval
- Positive Phase 3 results
- Positive Phase 2 results
- Breakthrough Therapy designation
- Fast Track designation
- Major pharmaceutical partnership
- Acquisition offer
- Buyout
- Major licensing agreement
- Strong efficacy results
- Successful clinical trial
- Major revenue contract

BULLISH:

- FDA meeting scheduled
- FDA acceptance of application
- Orphan Drug designation
- IND approval
- Trial enrollment completion
- Positive interim data
- New institutional investment
- Strategic partnership
- Conference presentation with potentially important data

NEUTRAL:

- Routine earnings
- Routine corporate updates
- Management changes
- Routine financing

BEARISH:

- Clinical trial failure
- FDA rejection
- FDA clinical hold
- Safety concerns
- Failed endpoint
- Offering/dilution
- Warrant exercise
- Reverse split
- Nasdaq delisting warning
- Going-concern warning

VERY BEARISH:

- Complete clinical failure
- FDA rejection of primary drug
- Serious safety signal
- Major fraud/accounting issue
- Bankruptcy
- Delisting

2. Why does it matter?
3. Is this genuinely new information?
