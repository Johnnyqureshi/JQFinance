Build/upgrade ONLY THE SEARCH PAGE of the existing trading/market intelligence website. Do not redesign or modify other pages, dashboards, trading bots, authentication, portfolio logic, or unrelated functionality.

OBJECTIVE

Turn the Search page into an advanced Market Intelligence Search Engine that combines:

1. Stock/ETF/company information
2. Market prices and technical data
3. News and catalysts
4. Federal Reserve / interest-rate expectations
5. Kalshi prediction-market probabilities
6. Macroeconomic expectations
7. Geopolitical/macro risk indicators
8. AI-generated interpretation of how these events could affect the searched asset

The goal is NOT to treat prediction markets as guaranteed forecasts. They should be presented as market-implied probabilities / crowd expectations and used as one input alongside price action, technicals, news, and macro data.
