- Higher High (HH) = bullish structural evidence

3. CLASSIFY SWING LOWS

- Higher Low (HL) = bullish structural evidence
- Lower Low (LL) = bearish structural evidence

- HH + HL

- Bullish structure should receive the strongest structural weighting

CONFIRMED BEARISH:
- LH + LL
- Price is failing below prior swing highs and/or breaking confirmed swing lows
- Bearish structure should receive the strongest structural weighting

MIXED / TRANSITIONING:
- HH + LL
- LH + HL
- Do NOT force a bullish or bearish classification from mixed structure.
- Clearly identify which structural condition is currently dominant.
