IMPORTANT: MODIFY ONLY THE SEARCH PAGE / STOCK DETAIL SEARCH EXPERIENCE.

DO NOT redesign or modify unrelated pages, trading bots, automation, broker integrations, portfolio functionality, authentication, database schemas, or existing trading logic outside of the Search Page.

The goal is to improve the Search Page's stock signal engine so that individual stock BUY/SELL signals are NEVER evaluated blindly without considering the broader market regime.

CURRENT PROBLEM:

The Search Page can currently show something like:

SPY → SELL / BEARISH
MSTR → BUY / BULLISH

This creates a misleading signal because the system is treating the individual ticker as if it exists independently of the overall market environment.

SPY represents the broad U.S. equity market and should be a major contextual input into every individual-stock signal.

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT implement the simplistic rule:

"SPY SELL = every stock SELL."

That is incorrect.

Instead, implement a MARKET REGIME → SECTOR/ASSET REGIME → INDIVIDUAL STOCK hierarchy.

The individual stock can still outperform a weak SPY, but the system must recognize that a BUY against a bearish market is a higher-risk, lower-confidence setup.

==================================================
1. CREATE A MARKET REGIME LAYER
==================================================

For every stock searched on the Search Page, first determine the current SPY market regime.

SPY should be classified into:

- STRONG BULLISH
- BULLISH
- NEUTRAL
- BEARISH
- STRONG BEARISH

Use the existing market/technical data already available to the application.

Consider, where data is available:

- SPY price trend
- short/intermediate moving averages
- moving-average alignment
- momentum
- RSI
- MACD
- volume direction
- support/resistance
- recent price structure
- volatility
- market breadth if already available
- gap behavior
- recent drawdown
- trend strength

Do NOT fabricate missing data.

If required market data is unavailable, explicitly mark it as unavailable rather than assuming bullish or bearish conditions.

==================================================
2. SPY MUST BECOME A MAJOR COMPONENT OF THE STOCK SIGNAL
==================================================

The stock's individual technical setup should remain important, but the market regime must materially affect the final signal.

Use a hierarchical model similar to:

MARKET REGIME
↓
SECTOR / ASSET CLASS REGIME
↓
INDIVIDUAL STOCK TECHNICAL SETUP
↓
FINAL SIGNAL

The final stock signal should therefore answer:

"Is this stock bullish?"

AND:

"Is this a favorable environment to buy this stock right now?"

These are NOT always the same thing.

==================================================
3. ADD MARKET ALIGNMENT TO THE SIGNAL ENGINE
==================================================

Add a "Market Alignment" factor to the stock scoring system.

Example scoring framework:

Individual Stock Setup: 50%
Market Regime: 25%
Sector / Industry Regime: 15%
Relative Strength vs SPY: 10%

The exact weighting can be adjusted based on the existing architecture, but market conditions MUST materially influence the final result.

Do not simply add points because SPY is green or subtract points because SPY is red.

Evaluate the actual regime.

==================================================
4. MARKET REGIME SHOULD MODIFY BUY SIGNALS
==================================================

Implement the following logic:

IF:

SPY = STRONG BULLISH
AND
Individual Stock = Bullish

→ Normal BUY can be allowed if all other conditions are satisfied.

IF:

SPY = BULLISH
AND
Individual Stock = Bullish

→ BUY / PROVISIONAL BUY depending on confidence.

IF:

SPY = NEUTRAL
AND
Individual Stock = Bullish

→ BUY can be allowed, but require stronger individual confirmation.

IF:

SPY = BEARISH
AND
Individual Stock = Bullish

→ DO NOT automatically show a normal BUY.

Instead:

→ downgrade the signal to:
"SPECULATIVE BUY"
or
"WAIT FOR CONFIRMATION"

depending on individual-stock strength.

IF:

SPY = STRONG BEARISH
AND
Individual Stock = Bullish

→ default to:

"WAIT"

unless the individual stock demonstrates exceptional relative strength and a very strong technical setup.

The system should make it obvious that this is a counter-trend trade.

==================================================
5. EXCEPTION: STRONG RELATIVE STRENGTH
==================================================

Do NOT force every stock to follow SPY.

Some stocks outperform during market weakness.

Therefore calculate:

STOCK PERFORMANCE VS SPY

and determine whether the stock is demonstrating meaningful relative strength.

A stock should only receive an exception to a bearish SPY environment if there is evidence such as:

- Strong relative strength vs SPY
- Price holding key support
- Higher highs / higher lows
- Strong volume
- Positive momentum
- Breakout confirmation
- Sector strength
- Stronger price structure than SPY
- Successful retest after breakout
- Institutional-style accumulation if reliable data exists

If the stock is merely green while SPY is red, THAT IS NOT ENOUGH.

A one-day positive move should not override a bearish market regime.

==================================================
6. SPECIAL HANDLING FOR HIGH-BETA / MARKET-SENSITIVE STOCKS
==================================================

Certain securities are especially sensitive to market risk.

Examples include:

- MSTR
- highly leveraged ETFs
- high-beta technology stocks
- speculative growth stocks
- crypto-related equities
- small caps
- momentum stocks

For these securities, a bearish SPY regime should carry MORE weight.

For example:

If:

SPY = BEARISH
MSTR = technically bullish

The system should NOT simply say:

"MSTR BUY"

Instead it should recognize:

"Individual setup bullish, but broader market regime bearish."

Possible final result:

SPECULATIVE BUY
HIGH RISK
WAIT FOR MARKET CONFIRMATION

or

WAIT

depending on confidence.

MSTR should also be evaluated against BTC/crypto conditions where existing data is available because MSTR is materially influenced by Bitcoin.

Do not assume MSTR behaves exactly like a normal equity.

==================================================
12. DO NOT CONFUSE MARKET DIRECTION WITH STOCK DIRECTION
==================================================

This distinction is extremely important.

SPY being bearish does NOT mean every stock must be bearish.

Instead:

SPY determines the MARKET ENVIRONMENT.

The individual stock determines its OWN technical setup.

The final signal combines both.

Think:

"Stock bullish + market bullish = higher-quality long setup."

"Stock bullish + market bearish = lower-quality / counter-trend setup."

"Stock bearish + market bearish = stronger bearish alignment."

"Stock bearish + market bullish = potentially weak stock, but market provides a tailwind."

18. FINAL PRINCIPLE
==================================================

The Search Page should answer TWO questions:

1. "Is this stock technically bullish or bearish?"

2. "Is this a favorable market environment for acting on that signal?"

The final signal should combine both answers.

The objective is NOT to predict that every stock follows SPY perfectly.

The objective is to prevent the system from issuing an overly confident BUY signal on an individual stock when the broader market environment is strongly unfavorable.

This is a RISK-AWARE SIGNAL ENGINE, not a simplistic SPY-following system.

Before making changes:
1. Inspect the existing Search Page signal-generation code.
2. Identify exactly where the individual stock BUY/SELL decision is generated.
3. Identify the existing SPY/market data available.
4. Integrate the market-regime layer into the existing architecture rather than creating duplicate systems.
5. Preserve all existing functionality.
6. Run tests/lint/build after implementation.
7. Report exactly which files/functions were modified and confirm that unrelated functionality was not changed.
