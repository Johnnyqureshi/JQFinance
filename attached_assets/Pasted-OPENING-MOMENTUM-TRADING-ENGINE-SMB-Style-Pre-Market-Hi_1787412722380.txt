OPENING MOMENTUM TRADING ENGINE

SMB-Style Pre-Market High Break Strategy

Implement this as a REAL-TIME trading strategy inside my existing trading bot.

The bot must be capable of operating in either PAPER or LIVE mode depending on the user’s broker/API configuration. Do NOT hard-code paper trading.

The strategy is designed for LONG opening-momentum trades.

==================================================

1. CORE STRATEGY
    ==================================================

Do NOT enter merely because:

* The stock is gapping up.
* The stock is green.
* The stock has news.
* The stock is breaking yesterday’s high.
* The stock touches the pre-market high.

The actual trigger is:

PRICE MUST CLOSE ABOVE THE PRE-MARKET HIGH ON A 2-MINUTE CANDLE WITH STRONG VOLUME EXPANSION.

The breakout must be confirmed by the candle CLOSE.

A wick above the pre-market high is NOT an entry.

==================================================
2. PRE-MARKET DATA

Before the market opens, calculate and store:

* Previous day’s close
* Previous day’s high
* Previous day’s low
* Pre-market high
* Pre-market low
* Pre-market volume
* Opening price
* Gap percentage
* ATR
* Relative volume
* Average historical volume by time of day
* 10 SMA
* 20 SMA
* 9 EMA on the 2-minute chart
* Current price
* Current volume
* Market/sector relative strength

The pre-market high must remain fixed as the primary breakout level after the regular session begins.

==================================================
3. INITIAL ELIGIBILITY GATE

A stock is eligible ONLY if:

1. OPENING PRICE > PREVIOUS DAY CLOSE

AND

2. OPENING PRICE > PREVIOUS DAY HIGH

If the stock opens below yesterday’s high:

DO NOT TRADE THIS SETUP.

Even if the stock subsequently breaks the pre-market high, reject the setup unless the strategy is explicitly configured to allow a secondary setup.

This is an important strategy filter.

==================================================
4. CATALYST REQUIREMENT

Prefer stocks with a genuine catalyst.

Examples:

* Earnings
* FDA/news
* Major company announcement
* Analyst upgrade/downgrade
* Contract
* Government announcement
* Industry/sector news
* Major technical event
* All-time-high breakout
* Significant daily-chart breakout

The catalyst should explain why abnormal buying interest exists.

If there is no obvious catalyst, the setup may still qualify if there is an exceptionally strong technical breakout and volume confirmation.

==================================================
5. BREAKOUT TRIGGER

Monitor 2-minute candles after the regular market opens.

ENTRY CONDITION:

A 2-minute candle must:

* Close ABOVE the pre-market high
* Have increased volume
* Demonstrate meaningful buying pressure
* Not be a weak/rejection candle

DO NOT ENTER when price merely touches or briefly trades above the pre-market high.

WAIT FOR THE 2-MINUTE CANDLE TO CLOSE.

If the candle closes above the pre-market high, generate an entry signal.

==================================================
6. VOLUME CONFIRMATION

Volume is a critical part of the strategy.

The bot must determine whether current volume is unusually strong relative to normal volume for that exact time of day.

Preferably calculate:

Relative Volume =
Current cumulative volume /
Average cumulative volume at the same time over the previous 5-10 trading sessions

Also calculate a standardized volume score where possible.

Strong confirmation should generally correspond to approximately:

3+ standard deviations above normal volume

OR

a clearly elevated relative-volume reading.

The bot must NOT use raw volume alone.

Example:

10:32 AM volume should be compared against typical 10:32 AM volume, NOT against full-day average volume.

==================================================
7. BREAKOUT CANDLE QUALITY FILTER

Reject the breakout if:

* Candle closes red
* Candle closes back below the pre-market high
* Upper wick is larger than approximately 50% of the candle body
* Breakout is only a wick
* Volume is weak
* Price immediately rejects the breakout level
* Spread becomes abnormally wide
* Liquidity is insufficient

The ideal breakout candle should show:

* Strong body
* Close near the high
* Strong volume
* Price acceptance above pre-market high

==================================================
8. OVERHEAD RESISTANCE FILTER

Before entering, calculate nearby resistance.

Look for:

* Previous daily highs
* Major intraday highs
* All-time highs
* Round numbers
* Premarket resistance
* Significant supply zones
* Prior consolidation levels

Avoid entering if obvious resistance is approximately 3-5% above the proposed entry unless the breakout is exceptionally strong.

Calculate:

Distance to nearest major resistance %

If insufficient room exists, reject the trade.

==================================================
9. MARKET FILTER

Check SPY and the relevant sector ETF/index.

Prefer long trades when:

* SPY is above its 10 SMA
* SPY is above its 20 SMA
* SPY has positive intraday momentum
* Relevant sector is strong
* Stock is outperforming the market

If SPY is strongly red or market conditions are hostile:

Reduce confidence or reject the setup depending on configuration.

The stock should ideally belong to a sector that is at least as strong as the broader market.

==================================================
10. RELATIVE STRENGTH

Calculate stock performance versus:

* SPY
* Relevant sector ETF

Example:

If SPY is +0.3% but the stock is +4%, the stock has strong relative strength.

Prefer stocks with significant relative strength.

==================================================
11. ENTRY

ENTRY PRICE:

The bot enters at or immediately after confirmation of the CLOSE of the qualifying 2-minute breakout candle.

Do NOT anticipate the breakout.

Do NOT buy at the pre-market high before confirmation.

Do NOT buy simply because the candle is trading above the pre-market high.

Confirmation first.

Then entry.

==================================================
12. STOP LOSS

Initial stop:

PLACE STOP BELOW THE LOW OF THE BREAKOUT 2-MINUTE CANDLE.

If price returns below the breakout candle low:

EXIT IMMEDIATELY.

Do not average down.

Do not widen the stop.

Do not move the stop farther away to avoid taking a loss.

The breakout must prove itself.

==================================================
13. RISK CALCULATION

Position size must be based on the distance between:

ENTRY PRICE

and

STOP PRICE.

Use:

Risk Per Share =
Entry - Stop

Position Size =
Maximum Dollar Risk / Risk Per Share

Allow the user to manually enter maximum dollar risk OR position size.

Do NOT automatically use a fixed number of shares regardless of stop distance.

If the stop distance is unusually large, automatically reduce share quantity.

If the calculated position size is below 1 share:

REJECT TRADE.

==================================================
14. RISK FILTER

Reject trades when:

* Stop is excessively wide
* Spread is too large
* Liquidity is inadequate
* Position size becomes impractical
* Risk/reward is poor
* Nearby resistance leaves insufficient upside
* Volume confirmation disappears

Never increase position size simply because the setup “looks good.”

==================================================
15. INITIAL PROFIT MANAGEMENT

After entry:

Monitor:

* ATR
* Previous resistance
* Round numbers
* Intraday highs
* Volume
* Momentum
* Distance from VWAP
* 9 EMA

Take partial profits into significant strength.

Allow the user to configure:

FIRST PROFIT TARGET
SECOND PROFIT TARGET
FINAL RUNNER MANAGEMENT

Default behavior:

Trim a portion of the position into a strong extension toward the next resistance/ATR target.

==================================================
16. RUNNER MANAGEMENT

For the remaining position:

Use the 2-minute 9 EMA as a trailing trend indicator.

If a 2-minute candle closes below the 9 EMA after the stock has extended:

Exit the runner.

Also exit if:

* 2-minute candle closes back below the pre-market high
* Momentum collapses
* Major reversal occurs
* Volume dries up while price fails to advance
* Stop is hit
* Market conditions deteriorate dramatically

==================================================
17. BREAKOUT FAILURE

A breakout is considered failed if:

Price closes back below the pre-market high after entering.

Treat this as an immediate warning.

Depending on configuration:

DEFAULT = EXIT rather than wait for the hard stop.

The bot must never turn a failed breakout into a long-term hold.

==================================================
18. TRADE TIME WINDOW

This is an OPENING MOMENTUM strategy.

Prioritize the first several minutes of regular trading.

Default:

Begin scanning immediately after market open.

Highest priority:

First 10-30 minutes.

Allow the user to configure the maximum entry time.

Example:

MAX_ENTRY_TIME = 10:30 AM ET

After the maximum entry time:

DO NOT initiate new trades using this opening-momentum setup.

Existing positions may continue to be managed.

==================================================
19. TRADE SCORE

Before placing an order, calculate a 0-100 setup score.

Suggested scoring:

Catalyst = 15 points
Opening gap / price positioning = 10
Above yesterday’s high = 10
Pre-market high breakout = 15
Volume expansion = 20
Breakout candle quality = 10
Relative strength = 5
Market strength = 5
Sector strength = 5
Room to resistance = 5

Maximum = 100.

DEFAULT:

Only execute trades with SCORE >= 80.

Allow user to change this threshold.

==================================================
20. ABSOLUTE NO-TRADE CONDITIONS

DO NOT TRADE if:

* Opening price is below yesterday’s high
* Breakout candle does not close above pre-market high
* Volume confirmation is missing
* Breakout candle is a major rejection
* Price immediately loses pre-market high
* Major resistance is too close
* Spread is excessive
* Liquidity is insufficient
* Risk cannot be calculated
* Stop distance is unreasonable
* Market conditions are extremely weak
* Data is stale or missing
* Broker/API confirmation is unavailable

When data is missing:

DO NOT GUESS.

Return:

“TRADE BLOCKED — REQUIRED DATA UNAVAILABLE.”
