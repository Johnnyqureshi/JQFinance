SMB-STYLE PRICE ACTION TRADING ENGINE

OBJECTIVE

Upgrade the existing trading bot into a professional-style intraday price-action engine inspired by the concepts presented by SMB Capital in:

“Price Action Trading Like an Elite Prop Trader (4 Killer Setups)”

Do NOT treat this as a simple indicator strategy.

The bot must evaluate:

1. Market context
2. Stock context
3. Price action
4. Relative strength/weakness
5. Key technical areas
6. Volume and momentum
7. Confirmation
8. Risk/reward
9. Trade management

The bot should only execute a trade when enough independent evidence supports a defined setup.

==================================================

1. MARKET CONTEXT

==================================================

Before entering any trade, determine:

* SPY trend
* QQQ trend
* IWM trend
* Market breadth if available
* Overall market momentum
* Market volatility
* Whether the market is trending, ranging, or transitioning

Classify the environment as:

RISK_ON
RISK_OFF
NEUTRAL
TRENDING
RANGING
HIGH_VOLATILITY

Use this classification to modify trade aggressiveness.

Do NOT automatically trade a stock simply because its individual chart looks attractive if the broader market strongly contradicts the trade.

==================================================

2. STOCK SELECTION

==================================================

Prioritize stocks that are:

* Highly liquid
* Actively traded
* Showing unusual volume
* Showing strong relative volume
* In play because of news, earnings, catalysts, sector movement, or unusual activity
* Showing meaningful intraday price movement
* Demonstrating clear relative strength or relative weakness

Calculate:

Relative Volume =
Current volume / Average volume for comparable time period

Give higher scores to stocks with significant relative volume.

Avoid low-liquidity stocks with unreliable spreads unless explicitly enabled by the user.

==================================================

3. IDENTIFY THE STOCK’S CHARACTER

==================================================

Determine whether the stock is:

STRONG
WEAK
NEUTRAL
TRENDING_UP
TRENDING_DOWN
RANGE_BOUND
EXTENDED
REVERSING

Compare the stock’s performance against:

* SPY
* QQQ
* Sector ETF
* Relevant industry peers when available

Example:

If SPY is weak but the stock is holding highs and repeatedly reclaiming VWAP, classify the stock as relatively strong.

If SPY is strong but the stock repeatedly fails at resistance and makes lower lows, classify it as relatively weak.

Relative strength/weakness is a major component of the setup score.

==================================================

4. KEY PRICE AREAS

==================================================

Identify important areas rather than relying on a single exact price.

Track:

* Premarket high
* Premarket low
* Previous day high
* Previous day low
* Previous close
* Opening price
* Intraday high
* Intraday low
* VWAP
* Anchored VWAP when available
* Major consolidation areas
* High-volume areas
* Recent swing highs
* Recent swing lows
* Support zones
* Resistance zones
* Psychological levels

Treat support and resistance as ZONES.

Do not assume a level is valid merely because price touched it once.

==================================================

5. PRICE ACTION ENGINE

==================================================

Analyze candle-by-candle behavior.

Track:

* Higher highs
* Higher lows
* Lower highs
* Lower lows
* Consolidation
* Expansion
* Compression
* Failed breakouts
* Failed breakdowns
* Reclaims
* Rejections
* Pullbacks
* Continuations
* Reversals

The bot must understand that the same price level can behave differently depending on:

* Momentum
* Volume
* Market environment
* Previous price action
* Number of tests
* Speed of approach
* Reaction after the level is breached

==================================================

6. SETUP A — BREAKOUT / BREAKDOWN

Identify a well-defined consolidation or resistance/support area.

LONG CONDITIONS:

1. Stock is showing relative strength.
2. Price forms a recognizable resistance zone.
3. Price consolidates near the upper portion of the range.
4. Volume begins increasing.
5. Price breaks above the resistance zone.
6. Breakout candle demonstrates meaningful expansion.
7. Breakout is confirmed rather than immediately rejected.
8. Market context does not strongly contradict the trade.

SHORT CONDITIONS:

Reverse the logic.

Look for:

* Relative weakness
* Consolidation near support
* Increasing selling pressure
* Breakdown through support
* Volume expansion
* Confirmation below the level

Do NOT chase an extremely extended breakout.

If price has already moved substantially beyond the breakout area, wait for a controlled pullback/retest.

==================================================

7. SETUP B — PULLBACK / CONTINUATION

Identify a strong directional move.

LONG:

1. Stock establishes an uptrend.
2. Strong impulse move occurs.
3. Stock pulls back.
4. Pullback volume decreases relative to the impulse.
5. Price holds an important support area/VWAP/previous breakout area.
6. Selling pressure begins to weaken.
7. Price resumes upward momentum.
8. Enter on confirmation.

SHORT:

Reverse the conditions.

Look for:

* Strong downtrend
* Sharp downside impulse
* Controlled bounce
* Weak buying volume
* Rejection at resistance/VWAP
* Resumption of downside movement

The bot should favor controlled pullbacks rather than buying extended candles.

==================================================

8. SETUP C — FAILED BREAKOUT / FAILED BREAKDOWN

A breakout that cannot hold can create a high-quality reversal opportunity.

LONG REVERSAL:

1. Price breaks below an important support area.
2. Breakdown fails.
3. Price quickly reclaims the level.
4. Selling pressure decreases.
5. Buyers regain control.
6. Volume confirms the reclaim.
7. Enter after confirmation.

SHORT REVERSAL:

1. Price breaks above resistance.
2. Breakout fails.
3. Price falls back below the resistance area.
4. Buyers become trapped.
5. Selling pressure increases.
6. Enter after confirmation.

The bot should explicitly detect trapped traders.

==================================================

9. SETUP D — EXTREME MOVE / REVERSION

Identify stocks that have become excessively extended from:

* VWAP
* Moving averages
* Recent support/resistance
* Intraday mean
* Recent consolidation

Do NOT automatically buy an oversold stock.

Do NOT automatically short an overbought stock.

Require evidence that the extreme move is actually reversing.

LONG REVERSAL CONFIRMATION:

* Extreme downside extension
* Selling momentum slows
* Price forms a higher low
* Important support holds
* Price reclaims a meaningful level
* Volume confirms buying
* Market/sector context supports the reversal

SHORT REVERSAL:

Reverse the conditions.

==================================================

10. VWAP

==================================================

Use VWAP as a contextual reference rather than a standalone buy/sell signal.

Track:

* Price above VWAP
* Price below VWAP
* VWAP slope
* VWAP reclaim
* VWAP rejection
* Distance from VWAP

Higher-quality LONG:

Price above VWAP + rising VWAP + relative strength + bullish price action.

Higher-quality SHORT:

Price below VWAP + falling VWAP + relative weakness + bearish price action.

A VWAP cross alone is NOT an entry signal.

==================================================

11. VOLUME ANALYSIS

==================================================

Volume must confirm price behavior.

Track:

* Relative volume
* Volume acceleration
* Volume expansion on breakout
* Volume contraction during pullback
* Volume expansion during reversal
* Volume divergence

Prefer:

Breakout + increasing volume

Pullback + decreasing volume

Reversal + renewed volume

Avoid:

Breakout + declining volume + immediate rejection.

==================================================

12. MOMENTUM

==================================================

Use momentum indicators as confirmation, NOT as the primary strategy.

The existing RSI functionality may remain available.

Use RSI to identify:

* Momentum
* Extreme conditions
* Divergence
* Potential exhaustion

Do NOT automatically buy RSI < 30.

Do NOT automatically short RSI > 70.

RSI should never override strong price-action evidence.

==================================================

13. TRADE SCORE

==================================================

Create a 0–100 setup score.

Suggested weighting:

Market Context             15 points
Stock Relative Strength    15 points
Price Action                20 points
Key Level                  10 points
Volume                     10 points
Momentum                   10 points
Setup Quality              10 points
Risk/Reward                10 points

Maximum = 100

Trade classifications:

90–100 = A+ setup
80–89  = A setup
70–79  = B setup
60–69  = Watch only
Below 60 = NO TRADE

Default automatic execution threshold:

80+

Allow the user to change this threshold.

==================================================

14. ENTRY CONFIRMATION

==================================================

Never enter merely because a setup appears possible.

Require confirmation.

Confirmation can include:

* Break of structure
* Reclaim
* Rejection
* Higher low
* Lower high
* Volume expansion
* VWAP reclaim/rejection
* Breakout confirmation
* Tape/Level 2 confirmation if available

If Level 2 is available, incorporate:

* Bid strength
* Ask pressure
* Spread
* Repeated absorption
* Aggressive buyers
* Aggressive sellers
* Failed pushes

If Level 2 is unavailable, continue using price/volume data.

==================================================

15. STOP LOSS

==================================================

Stops must be based on the trade thesis.

Do NOT use an arbitrary percentage stop for every trade.

Examples:

Breakout trade:
Stop below breakout/retest structure.

Pullback trade:
Stop below the pullback’s structural low.

Short continuation:
Stop above structural lower high.

Failed breakout short:
Stop above failed breakout area.

Reversal long:
Stop below reversal structure/support.

The bot must calculate:

Risk per share =
Entry price - Stop price

For shorts:

Risk per share =
Stop price - Entry price

==================================================

16. RISK / REWARD

==================================================

Before executing:

Calculate realistic target based on:

* Previous high/low
* Support/resistance
* Measured move
* VWAP
* Consolidation range
* Volatility
* ATR when available

Default minimum acceptable reward/risk:

2.0 : 1

Preferred:

2.5 : 1 or better

Do not enter if the nearest realistic target does not provide adequate reward relative to the stop.

==================================================

17. PROFIT MANAGEMENT

==================================================

Do not automatically exit solely because RSI reaches 70/75.

Instead:

Take partial profits near meaningful resistance/target zones.

After price moves favorably:

* Protect gains
* Raise stop when justified
* Trail behind higher lows for longs
* Trail above lower highs for shorts

Allow the user to configure:

FIRST_TARGET_R
SECOND_TARGET_R
TRAILING_STOP
PARTIAL_EXIT_PERCENT

Example:

At +1R:
Optionally take 25%.

At +2R:
Optionally take another 25–50%.

Allow remaining shares to run while structure remains favorable.

==================================================

18. TRADE INVALIDATION

==================================================

Immediately reassess the trade when:

* Breakout fails
* VWAP is lost after a VWAP-dependent setup
* Support breaks
* Resistance rejects unexpectedly
* Volume disappears
* Market conditions change
* Relative strength/weakness reverses
* Original trade thesis is no longer valid

Do not hold a trade simply because the position is losing.

The question is:

“Is the original trade thesis still valid?”

If NO:

Exit.

==================================================

19. NO-TRADE CONDITIONS

==================================================

Do NOT trade when:

* Market is extremely choppy
* Spread is excessive
* Liquidity is insufficient
* Volume is abnormally low
* Setup is unclear
* Risk/reward is poor
* Price is already excessively extended
* Multiple signals contradict one another
* The setup cannot be objectively defined
* The stock is halted or trading abnormally
* Data quality is unreliable

“No trade” is a valid and preferred decision.

==================================================

21. POSITION MANAGEMENT

==================================================

Once in a position:

Continuously monitor:

* Price
* Volume
* VWAP
* Structure
* Relative strength
* Market direction
* Target proximity
* Stop proximity

Never increase position size simply because the trade is losing.

Only add to a position if:

1. The strategy explicitly permits scaling.
2. The original thesis remains valid.
3. The new entry provides favorable risk/reward.
4. Total risk remains within the user’s maximum risk limit.

==================================================

22. MARKET REGIME ADAPTATION

==================================================

TRENDING MARKET:

Favor:

* Breakouts
* Pullbacks
* Continuations
* Relative-strength trades

RANGING MARKET:

Favor:

* Reversions
* Failed breakouts
* Failed breakdowns
* Range-edge trades

HIGH VOLATILITY:

Reduce aggressiveness.

Require stronger confirmation.

Widen structural stops only when justified by volatility and reduce position size accordingly.

==================================================

23. POSITION SIZING

==================================================

Do not hard-code position size.

Respect the user’s existing manual position-size setting.

If risk-based sizing is enabled:

Position Size =
Maximum Dollar Risk / Risk Per Share

Never exceed:

* User-defined max position
* User-defined max dollar risk
* Broker buying power
* Account risk limits

PAPER mode

Use appropriate order types based on the existing architecture.

25. SCANNER

==================================================

Continuously scan the user’s stock universe.

Prioritize stocks with:

* High relative volume
* Large percentage move
* Strong price momentum
* News/catalyst
* Significant premarket movement
* VWAP interaction

SETUP SCORE

==================================================

26. HUMAN-READABLE TRADE EXPLANATION

==================================================

For every potential trade, produce a concise explanation:

“LONG — A setup

Stock is showing relative strength versus SPY, holding above VWAP, consolidating below resistance, and volume is increasing. A confirmed breakout above the resistance zone provides the entry. Structural stop is below the consolidation. First target is the next resistance area. Estimated R:R = X.”

For a rejected trade:

“NO TRADE — Setup score 67/100. Breakout lacks volume confirmation and expected reward does not justify the risk.”

==================================================

27. IMPORTANT PRINCIPLE

The bot must NOT attempt to predict the future with certainty.

It should react to what price is actually doing.

Think:

“If X happens, do Y.”

NOT:

“Price will definitely go up.”

Every trade must have:

SETUP
TRIGGER
ENTRY
STOP
TARGET
INVALIDATION

If the trigger never occurs:

NO TRADE.

FINAL OPERATING RULE

The bot’s job is NOT to trade constantly.

The bot’s job is to identify:

HIGH-QUALITY
DEFINED
CONFIRMED
FAVORABLE-RISK/REWARD

trade opportunities.

When the market does not provide one:

WAIT.

A missed trade is preferable to an undefined trade.

Build the system so every executed trade can be explained in plain English and every trade can later be reviewed against the exact setup rules that caused the execution.
