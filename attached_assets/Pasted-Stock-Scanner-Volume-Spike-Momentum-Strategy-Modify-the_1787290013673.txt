Stock Scanner & Volume-Spike Momentum Strategy

Modify the stock-analysis/trading bot to continuously scan for stocks matching the following Finviz-style criteria and automatically monitor them for a bullish momentum entry.

1. INITIAL STOCK UNIVERSE FILTER

Only consider stocks satisfying:

* Short Float: > 30%
* Relative Volume: > 1.5
* Shares Outstanding: < 20 million
* Optionable/Shortable: Optional — do not require this filter
* Exchange: Any
* Market Cap: Any
* Price: Any
* Country: USA preferred, but do not hard-reject other exchanges unless the broker/data feed cannot trade them

The purpose of these filters is to find low-share-count stocks with significant short interest and unusual trading activity.

2. REAL-TIME VOLUME MONITORING

After a stock passes the initial filters, continuously monitor:

* Current volume
* Relative volume
* Volume acceleration
* 1-minute volume
* 5-minute volume
* Current price
* Price change %
* VWAP
* RSI
* Bid/ask if available
* High of day
* Premarket high if applicable

Do NOT enter simply because relative volume is >1.5.

The bot must detect a NEW volume spike developing in real time.

A volume spike should be considered significant when current short-term volume is substantially greater than the stock’s recent average volume.

Use configurable thresholds:

VOLUME_SPIKE_MULTIPLIER = 2.0
RELATIVE_VOLUME_MIN = 1.5

The bot should allow these values to be changed from the strategy settings.

3. BULLISH ENTRY CONFIRMATION

When a qualifying stock experiences a volume spike, place it into a “MOMENTUM WATCH” state.

Do not immediately buy.

Wait for bullish price confirmation.

A valid long entry should preferably satisfy:

1. Volume spike detected.
2. Price is moving upward rather than downward.
3. Current price is above VWAP OR reclaims VWAP with increasing volume.
4. Current candle closes bullish.
5. Price breaks above the recent short-term high/resistance.
6. Volume remains elevated during the breakout.
7. RSI is NOT already at or above 75.

The bot should prioritize entries where volume expansion and price expansion happen together.

4. ENTRY TRIGGER

The preferred entry is:

VOLUME SPIKE
+
BULLISH PRICE MOVEMENT
+
BREAKOUT/RECLAIM
+
CONTINUING VOLUME

Example:

Stock is trading at $5.00.

Recent volume is normal.

A sudden volume surge begins.

Price moves:

$5.00 → $5.08 → $5.15

The recent short-term resistance is $5.12.

If price breaks $5.12 with elevated volume and confirms the move, generate a LONG ENTRY signal.

Do not chase a stock after an extreme vertical move.

Use a configurable maximum chase percentage.

MAX_CHASE_PERCENT = 3%

If price has already moved more than this percentage above the identified breakout level, wait for a pullback/retest rather than entering immediately.

5. PULLBACK / RETEST ENTRY

If the initial breakout is missed, do not automatically abandon the stock.

Watch for:

* Pullback toward breakout level
* Pullback toward VWAP
* Volume decreasing during the pullback
* Bullish volume returning
* Price reclaiming the breakout level

If the stock successfully retests the breakout area and begins moving upward again with increasing volume, allow a secondary LONG entry.

6. RSI TAKE-PROFIT

Continuously calculate RSI using a configurable period.

Default:

RSI_PERIOD = 14

Primary take-profit trigger:

TAKE_PROFIT_RSI = 75

When RSI reaches or exceeds 75, begin taking profit.

Default behavior:

IF RSI >= 75:
EXIT LONG POSITION

Make this configurable.

The bot should NOT wait for RSI to fall back below 75 before exiting.

The purpose is to capture the momentum while the stock is becoming overbought.

7. ADDITIONAL RISK PROTECTION

Never enter a stock solely because:

* Short float >30%
* Relative volume >1.5
* Shares outstanding <20M

Those conditions only identify the candidate.

The actual trade requires real-time bullish price/volume confirmation.

Add configurable protection for:

* Maximum spread
* Maximum chase percentage
* Maximum daily loss
* Maximum number of simultaneous positions
* Stop loss
* Trading halt detection
* Data-feed failure
* Stale quotes
* Sudden liquidity deterioration

If the bid/ask spread becomes abnormally wide, do not enter.

8. STATE MACHINE

Every candidate should move through these states:

WATCHLIST
↓
VOLUME_SPIKE
↓
MOMENTUM_WATCH
↓
BREAKOUT_CONFIRMATION
↓
LONG_ENTRY
↓
POSITION_MONITOR
↓
RSI >= 75
↓
TAKE_PROFIT
↓
CLOSED

If momentum disappears before entry, return the stock to WATCHLIST.

Do not repeatedly enter and exit the same stock because of noisy signals. Add a configurable cooldown period after a rejected signal or closed trade.

9. REAL-TIME SCANNING LOOP

The scanner should continuously:

1. Retrieve the current stock universe.
2. Apply the Finviz-style filters.
3. Monitor qualifying stocks in real time.
4. Detect abnormal volume.
5. Detect bullish price acceleration.
6. Identify breakout levels.
7. Wait for confirmation.
8. Generate a LONG ENTRY signal.
9. Monitor the position.
10. Exit when RSI >= 75 or another configured risk condition occurs.

Do not rely on a once-per-minute static scan if the data provider supports streaming or websocket data.

Use real-time streaming data whenever available.

12. IMPORTANT

Do not hard-code a particular stock.

Do not automatically buy simply because a stock appears in the scanner.

The scanner identifies the opportunity.

The trading engine waits for real-time confirmation that:

UNUSUAL VOLUME
+
PRICE STRENGTH
+
BREAKOUT/RECLAIM

LONG ENTRY

Then manage the position until:

RSI >= 75

or a configured risk-management condition occurs.

Keep the existing broker/API architecture and existing paper/live trading selection intact. Do not remove any existing trading functionality. Make this strategy modular so it can be enabled or disabled from the strategy settings.
