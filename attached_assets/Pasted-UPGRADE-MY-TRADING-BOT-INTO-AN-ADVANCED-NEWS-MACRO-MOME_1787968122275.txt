==================================================

Build a scanner specifically designed to find stocks experiencing unusual volume.

Relative Volume (RVOL) =
Current/Recent volume compared with the stock's historical average volume for the equivalent period.

Prioritize:

RVOL >= 1.5 = interesting
RVOL >= 2.0 = strong
RVOL >= 3.0 = very strong
RVOL >= 5.0 = exceptional

- Dollar volume

Do not rank a stock highly based on RVOL alone.
