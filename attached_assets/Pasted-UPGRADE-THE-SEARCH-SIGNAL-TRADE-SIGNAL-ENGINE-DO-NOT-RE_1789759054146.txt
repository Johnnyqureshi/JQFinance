UPGRADE THE SEARCH SIGNAL / TRADE SIGNAL ENGINE — DO NOT REDESIGN THE EXISTING UI

The current signal logic is misleading because it can issue a BUY signal after a stock has already made a large move higher, simply because price structure, momentum, and volume are bullish.

Example:
MSTR can be up +15% in one session, trading near resistance, RSI overbought, and significantly extended from moving averages, yet the system currently says:
BUY — 60% confidence.

That is NOT acceptable.

Keep the existing Qureshi Finance design, layout, cards, colors, support/resistance section, gap section, and all existing functionality. ONLY upgrade the underlying signal/entry logic.

NEW CORE PRINCIPLE:

Separate:
1. TREND DIRECTION
2. MOMENTUM
3. ENTRY QUALITY
4. RESISTANCE/OVERHEAD SUPPLY
5. VOLUME CONFIRMATION
6. EXTENSION / CHASE RISK
7. MARKET/MACRO CONTEXT

A bullish trend does NOT automatically mean BUY NOW.

==================================================
1. TREND ≠ ENTRY
==================================================

The engine must distinguish between:

BULLISH TREND:
Price structure is bullish, higher highs/higher lows, moving averages aligned bullishly, etc.

BUYABLE ENTRY:
The current price still offers a technically favorable entry.

A stock can be:
BULLISH + STRONG MOMENTUM + POOR ENTRY

In that situation, DO NOT issue a high-confidence BUY.

The signal must evaluate whether the current price is already extended.

==================================================
2. ADD AN "ENTRY QUALITY" ENGINE
==================================================

Calculate an Entry Quality Score independently from the Trend Score.

Evaluate:

- Distance from nearest support
- Distance from nearest resistance
- Distance from 20 SMA
- Distance from 50 SMA
- Distance from 200 SMA
- ATR-based extension
- Percentage gain today
- Percentage gain over 3 sessions
- Percentage gain over 5 sessions
- RSI
- Volume expansion
- Relative volume
- Distance from recent breakout level
- Whether price has already broken out significantly
- Whether price is retesting a breakout
- Whether price is directly underneath resistance

Do NOT allow bullish trend alone to create a BUY.

==================================================
3. PENALIZE CHASING
==================================================

Add a strong "CHASE RISK" calculation.

When a stock has made an abnormal move upward in a short period, reduce BUY confidence.

Examples:

+8% or more in one session:
Begin reducing entry quality.

+12% or more:
Significant chase-risk penalty.

+15% or more:
Strong chase-risk penalty unless there is a confirmed breakout with exceptional sustained volume and adequate room to the next resistance.

Also calculate:

distance_from_20SMA
distance_from_50SMA
distance_from_200SMA
ATR_extension

If price is unusually far above these averages, the engine should recognize that the trend may be bullish while the immediate entry is poor.

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT automatically label the stock bearish just because it is extended.

The correct interpretation is:

TREND = BULLISH
ENTRY = EXTENDED

==================================================
4. RESISTANCE MUST MATTER
==================================================

The closer price gets to meaningful resistance, the more the system should reduce immediate BUY confidence.

Calculate:

distance_to_resistance_percent

If price is:

>10% below resistance:
Normal.

5–10% below:
Caution.

2–5% below:
Reduce entry quality.

<2% below:
Major resistance proximity penalty.

At/above resistance:
The system must determine whether this is a TRUE BREAKOUT or simply a rejection risk.

Never issue a high-confidence BUY merely because price is bullish while sitting directly underneath major resistance.

==================================================
5. BREAKOUT LOGIC
==================================================

A breakout should require multiple confirmations.

A TRUE BULLISH BREAKOUT requires:

- Price above resistance
- Meaningful volume expansion
- Relative volume confirmation
- Candle closes above resistance
- No immediate rejection back below resistance
- Preferably sustained trading above the breakout level
- Adequate room toward the next resistance

Do NOT classify an intraday spike above resistance as a confirmed breakout.

If price briefly trades above resistance but falls back underneath it:

BREAKOUT = FAILED / UNCONFIRMED

and reduce BUY confidence.

==================================================
6. VOLUME LOGIC
==================================================

Volume must be interpreted correctly.

High volume by itself does NOT equal BUY.

Determine whether volume is:

A. Confirming the move
B. Exhaustion volume
C. Selling/distribution volume
D. Breakout volume
E. Fading volume

Example:

Stock +15%
Volume +100%
Price approaching resistance
RSI >75
Price far above 20 SMA

This should NOT automatically produce BUY.

The system should recognize:

STRONG MOMENTUM + HIGH VOLUME + HIGH EXTENSION = POSSIBLE EXHAUSTION / CHASE RISK

Volume should increase confidence only when it confirms a technically favorable setup.

==================================================
7. RSI / OVERBOUGHT LOGIC
==================================================

Do NOT use RSI as a simplistic sell indicator.

Instead:

RSI 50–70:
Healthy bullish momentum.

RSI 70–75:
Strong momentum but increasingly extended.

RSI 75–80:
Major extension penalty for NEW entries.

RSI >80:
Severe entry-extension penalty.

An overbought stock can continue higher.

Therefore:

OVERBOUGHT ≠ BEARISH

Instead:

OVERBOUGHT = REDUCED NEW-ENTRY QUALITY

==================================================
8. MOVING AVERAGE EXTENSION
==================================================

Calculate percentage distance from:

20 SMA
50 SMA
200 SMA

The farther price becomes from its moving averages, the more the system should reduce immediate entry quality.

Do not interpret this as bearish.

Example:

Price > 20 SMA > 50 SMA > 200 SMA

Trend = bullish.

But if price is dramatically extended above 20 SMA:

Entry quality = reduced.

==================================================
9. SUPPORT / RESISTANCE QUALITY
==================================================

Do NOT invent precise-looking levels without explaining their source.

Every support/resistance level must have a methodology.

Possible sources:

- Recent swing highs/lows
- Major pivots
- Previous breakout levels
- High-volume price areas
- Moving averages
- Previous daily highs/lows
- Significant gap boundaries
- Multi-touch levels

Store the source/type of each level.

Example:

$161.73
Resistance
Source: previous swing high + volume structure

NOT simply:

$161.73 Resistance

if the system cannot explain where it came from.

==================================================
10. GAP LOGIC
==================================================

Only call something an "OPEN DAILY PRICE GAP" if it is actually an unfilled historical gap based on the underlying OHLC data.

For every gap calculate:

- Gap range
- Gap date
- Direction
- Distance from current price
- Historical fill rate
- Sample size

Do not display a fill percentage unless it was actually calculated from historical data.

Always display:

"Historical fill rate — not a prediction"

Do not treat gap-fill probability as a guaranteed future outcome.

==================================================
11. CONFIDENCE SCORE
==================================================

The current 60% BUY confidence is too easy to generate.

Confidence must represent the quality of the CURRENT SETUP, not simply how bullish the trend is.

Example:

TREND SCORE = 90/100
MOMENTUM SCORE = 95/100
ENTRY QUALITY = 45/100
CHASE RISK = HIGH
RESISTANCE PROXIMITY = HIGH

Final signal should NOT be 90% BUY.

It should reflect the poor entry.

Use weighted scoring:

Trend: 25%
Momentum: 15%
Volume: 15%
Entry Quality: 20%
Support/Resistance: 10%
Extension/Chase Risk: 10%
Market/Macro Context: 5%

The final confidence must be mathematically derived from these components.

==================================================
12. ADD SIGNAL STATES
==================================================

Use these states:

STRONG BUY
BUY
BULLISH — EXTENDED
BREAKOUT WATCH
NEUTRAL
BEARISH
STRONG SELL

Most importantly:

"BULLISH — EXTENDED"

should be available when:

- Overall trend is bullish
- Momentum is strong
- But price is too extended for a favorable new entry

This is preferable to falsely calling the stock a BUY.

Example:

MSTR
BULLISH — EXTENDED
Trend: Bullish
Momentum: Strong
Volume: Confirming
Entry Quality: Low
Chase Risk: High
Resistance: $161.73
Current Price: $152.76

This tells the user what is actually happening.

==================================================
13. DO NOT HIDE THE REASON
==================================================

Every signal must explain BOTH sides.

Example:

"Bullish trend and strong volume support upside, but price is extended +15% today and is approaching major resistance. Immediate entry quality is reduced."

Do not only show:

"Price structure and volume direction support buyers."

That is incomplete and misleading.

A stock can remain technically bullish while having elevated market risk.

Do NOT use language implying:

"Strong momentum = BUY"

Instead:

"Strong momentum supports the trend, but entry quality depends on location."

This distinction is mandatory throughout the signal engine.

BULLISH — EXTENDED

BREAKOUT WATCH

==================================================
FINAL OBJECTIVE
==================================================

The purpose of this upgrade is to prevent Qureshi Finance from telling users to BUY a stock simply because it is already pumping.

The engine must answer two separate questions:

"Is this asset bullish?"

AND

"Is NOW a technically favorable place to enter?"

Those are NOT the same question.

A stock can be extremely bullish and still be a bad immediate entry because it is extended, overbought, approaching resistance, or experiencing potential exhaustion.

Implement this logic without breaking any existing functionality.
