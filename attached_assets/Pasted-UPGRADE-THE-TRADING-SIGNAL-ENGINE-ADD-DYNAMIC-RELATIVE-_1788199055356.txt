UPGRADE THE TRADING SIGNAL ENGINE — ADD DYNAMIC RELATIVE VOLUME / PUMP DETECTION

The current analyzer must NOT issue a SELL simply because:
- RSI is elevated
- Price is near resistance
- The current candle/day is red
- Price is above/below a moving average

Those signals must be evaluated together with REAL-TIME RELATIVE VOLUME and volume acceleration.

PRIMARY NEW VARIABLE: RELATIVE VOLUME (RVOL)

Calculate:

RVOL = Current Volume / Average Volume for the same time period

For intraday trading, DO NOT compare current volume to the full-day average. Use time-of-day normalized volume.

Calculate Expected Volume At This Time:
ExpectedVolume = historical average cumulative volume at the current minute of the trading session.

RVOL = Current Cumulative Volume / ExpectedVolume

Also calculate:

VolumeAcceleration =
Current short-term volume rate / Previous short-term volume rate

Use 1-minute, 5-minute and 15-minute volume where available.

PUMP / BREAKOUT DETECTION

Create a dynamic PUMP PRESSURE SCORE from 0–100.

Increase the score when:

1. RVOL > 1.5
2. RVOL > 2.0 = strong volume confirmation
3. RVOL > 3.0 = extreme volume
4. VolumeAcceleration > 1.25
5. Price is making higher lows
6. Price is breaking previous intraday highs
7. Price is approaching resistance while volume is increasing
8. Price breaks resistance with expanding volume
9. Bid/ask or trade-flow data indicates aggressive buying, if available
10. Price is rising while volume is increasing

IMPORTANT:
A resistance test accompanied by increasing RVOL should NOT automatically produce SELL.

Instead classify it as:

"BREAKOUT WATCH"

when:
- Price is within approximately 1% of resistance
- RVOL > 1.5
- VolumeAcceleration > 1.10
- Price structure is bullish

Classify as:

"BREAKOUT CONFIRMED"

when:
- Price breaks resistance
- Breakout candle volume is at least 1.5x expected volume
- RVOL is increasing rather than decreasing
- Price remains above the breakout level
- Follow-through occurs on subsequent candles

BEARISH VOLUME DETECTION

Increase SELL pressure when:

- Price is falling AND RVOL is increasing
- Price breaks support AND volume expands
- Volume expands on red candles and contracts on green candles
- Lower highs/lower lows are developing
- Resistance rejection occurs with heavy selling volume

Do NOT interpret high volume by itself as bearish.
High volume means PARTICIPATION.
The direction of price movement determines whether the volume is bullish or bearish.

CREATE A VOLUME/PRICE CONFIRMATION SCORE

Calculate:

BullishVolumeScore 0–100
BearishVolumeScore 0–100

BullishVolumeScore increases when:
- Price ↑ + Volume ↑
- Breakout + high RVOL
- Higher low + increasing volume
- Buying acceleration
- Resistance approaching + volume expansion

BearishVolumeScore increases when:
- Price ↓ + Volume ↑
- Support breakdown + high RVOL
- Lower high + increasing selling volume
- Rejection from resistance + heavy volume

SIGNAL PRIORITY

The final signal must combine:

RSI
Trend
Support/Resistance
Price Structure
Relative Volume
Volume Acceleration
Breakout/Breakdown Confirmation
Momentum

Do NOT allow RSI or resistance alone to generate SELL.

Example:

RSI = 60.8
Price = near resistance
Current day = red
BUT:
RVOL = 2.4
VolumeAcceleration = 1.45
Price = making higher lows
Price = moving toward resistance

The system should NOT simply say SELL.

It should say something similar to:

"BREAKOUT WATCH — HIGH VOLUME"
Confidence: 72%
RVOL: 2.4x
Volume acceleration: +45%
Resistance: $XXX
Reason: Significant volume is entering while price is pressing resistance. A breakout is possible if resistance is reclaimed with continued volume.

If resistance breaks with volume confirmation:
Upgrade to BUY / BREAKOUT CONFIRMED.

If price rejects resistance AND selling volume expands:
Downgrade to SELL.

DYNAMIC CONFIDENCE

The confidence score must continuously recalculate whenever new market data arrives.

Do NOT use hardcoded confidence such as:
"SELL 60%"

Instead calculate confidence from the weighted evidence.

Suggested weighting:

Relative Volume / Volume Flow: 25%
Price Structure / Momentum: 25%
Support & Resistance: 20%
Trend: 15%
RSI: 10%
Other indicators: 5%

Most importantly, allow volume and price action to OVERRIDE weaker conflicting indicators.

The analyzer's objective is NOT to predict the future with certainty.

Its objective is to detect when real-time volume and price behavior are creating an abnormal probability of a breakout, continuation, reversal, or breakdown.
