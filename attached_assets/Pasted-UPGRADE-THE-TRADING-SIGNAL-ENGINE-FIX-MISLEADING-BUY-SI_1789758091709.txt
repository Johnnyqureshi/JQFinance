UPGRADE THE TRADING SIGNAL ENGINE — FIX MISLEADING BUY SIGNALS AT RESISTANCE

The current signal logic is misleading because it can produce a high-confidence BUY while price is sitting directly underneath major resistance. A bullish trend does NOT automatically mean BUY.

DO NOT redesign the UI.
DO NOT remove existing functionality.
DO NOT change the current support/resistance display.
Upgrade ONLY the signal-generation and confidence logic.

CORE PRINCIPLE:

SEPARATE:
1. MARKET/TREND BIAS
2. TRADE ENTRY QUALITY
3. BREAKOUT CONFIRMATION

A bullish trend can exist while the correct action is NO BUY because price is extended and pressing resistance.

==================================================
1. RESISTANCE PROXIMITY MUST MATTER
==================================================

Never issue a high-confidence BUY when price is immediately below a significant resistance level.

Calculate:

distance_to_resistance_percent =
((nearest_resistance - current_price) / current_price) * 100

Classify:

> 3.0% below resistance:
Normal room for upside.

1.5%–3.0% below resistance:
Caution.

0.5%–1.5% below resistance:
Resistance zone. Require strong confirmation.

< 0.5% below resistance:
DO NOT issue a normal BUY unless an actual breakout is confirmed.

If current price is directly underneath resistance, the system must recognize:

"Bullish trend ≠ bullish entry."

==================================================
2. DO NOT BUY INTO RESISTANCE
==================================================

If:

price < resistance
AND
price is within 1.5% of resistance
AND
resistance has NOT been broken

then the signal cannot be a high-confidence BUY.

The system should instead identify:

"BULLISH STRUCTURE — RESISTANCE TEST"

or

"WAIT FOR BREAKOUT CONFIRMATION"

Do NOT artificially assign a 70%+ BUY confidence in this situation.

==================================================
3. VOLUME MUST CONFIRM THE BREAKOUT
==================================================

Volume should not simply be described as "increasing."

Compare:

- Current volume
- 20-period average volume
- Volume during previous resistance tests
- Spot volume where available
- Futures volume where available

A breakout is stronger when:

price breaks resistance
+
volume expands materially above average
+
spot volume participates
+
breakout candle closes above resistance
+
price holds above resistance on subsequent candles

If price approaches resistance while volume is declining:

NEGATIVE ENTRY FACTOR.

If price repeatedly tests resistance without increasing volume:

NEGATIVE ENTRY FACTOR.

Do not call fading volume "increasing volume."

==================================================
4. DISTINGUISH LIQUIDATION VOLUME FROM ORGANIC BUYING
==================================================

For BTC/crypto:

Do NOT treat futures liquidation volume as equivalent to organic spot buying.

If a major price move is accompanied by:

- Large short liquidations
- Rapid futures volume expansion
- Open-interest changes
- Funding changes
- Weak spot participation

then classify the move as potentially squeeze-driven.

This should REDUCE breakout confidence.

Example:

+6% price move
+ huge futures volume
+ large short liquidations
+ price stalls underneath resistance

= DO NOT issue a high-confidence BUY.

Instead:

"BULLISH PRICE ACTION — SQUEEZE-DRIVEN BREAKOUT NOT CONFIRMED"

==================================================
5. BREAKOUT CONFIRMATION
==================================================

A resistance breakout should require confirmation.

Minimum confirmation:

1. Price closes above resistance.
2. Volume is above its recent average.
3. Volume is not rapidly fading.
4. Breakout is not immediately rejected.
5. Price remains above the former resistance level.
6. Prefer confirmation from multiple timeframes.

Only after these conditions are satisfied should the system upgrade the BUY signal.

A wick above resistance is NOT a breakout.

A temporary intraday move above resistance is NOT automatically a breakout.

A liquidation spike above resistance is NOT automatically a breakout.

==================================================
6. FAILED BREAKOUT / BULL TRAP DETECTION
==================================================

Create a dedicated BULL TRAP condition.

Trigger when:

price moves above resistance
BUT
fails to hold above resistance
AND
closes back below resistance

Increase bull-trap risk further when:

- Volume fades after breakout
- Open interest falls sharply
- Short liquidations were unusually large
- Spot volume does not confirm
- Price loses the breakout level quickly
- Momentum divergence appears
- Broader market is weakening
- SPY is weakening
- VIX is rising
- Treasury yields are rising
- Oil is rising sharply
- Macro risk is increasing

Do NOT immediately return BUY after a failed breakout.

==================================================
7. RANGE POSITION MUST BE PART OF THE SIGNAL
==================================================

Determine where price sits inside the current trading range.

Calculate:

range_position =
(current_price - range_low) /
(range_high - range_low)

Interpretation:

0–25%:
Lower portion of range.
Better location for evaluating long setups.

25–50%:
Middle/lower-middle range.

50–75%:
Upper-middle range.
Require stronger confirmation.

75–100%:
Upper portion of range / near resistance.
Do NOT automatically chase.

If price is in the upper 20–25% of the range and approaching resistance, substantially reduce BUY confidence unless breakout confirmation exists.

IMPORTANT:

The same bullish setup should NOT receive the same BUY confidence at the bottom of a range and at the top of a range.

==================================================
8. RISK/REWARD MUST BE CHECKED BEFORE BUY
==================================================

Before issuing BUY:

Calculate:

distance_to_support
distance_to_resistance

Then calculate approximate upside versus downside.

If resistance is very close and support is significantly farther away, do NOT produce a high-confidence BUY.

Example:

Current price = $80,984
Resistance = $81,433
Support = $79,818

The upside to resistance is tiny compared with the downside to support.

Therefore:

DO NOT interpret this as a 70% BUY.

It should be classified as:

"BULLISH STRUCTURE — POOR ENTRY LOCATION"

until resistance breaks.

==================================================
9. MACRO REGIME MUST MODIFY ENTRY CONFIDENCE
==================================================

For BTC and risk assets, incorporate:

- Fed policy
- Global central-bank policy
- Treasury yields
- Dollar strength
- Oil
- Inflation
- Liquidity/M2
- SPY trend
- VIX
- Geopolitical risk
- Credit conditions
- Major macroeconomic releases

Macro should NOT override price action, but it must modify confidence.

Example:

Bullish technical structure
+
price directly under resistance
+
fading volume
+
rising yields
+
elevated oil
+
tightening global monetary conditions

= NOT a high-confidence BUY.

==================================================
10. SPY / MARKET REGIME FILTER
==================================================

For individual stocks and crypto:

If SPY is strongly bearish or losing important support, reduce long-entry confidence.

Do not allow an individual asset to receive an extremely high BUY confidence merely because its own chart is temporarily bullish when the broader risk environment is deteriorating.

The system should distinguish:

"Asset-specific strength"

from

"Broad risk-on confirmation."

==================================================
11. CONFIDENCE SCORE MUST REPRESENT ENTRY QUALITY
==================================================

The confidence percentage must represent the probability/quality of the CURRENT ENTRY SETUP.

It must NOT simply represent:

"How bullish the trend looks."

A strong bullish trend at resistance can have:

BULLISH TREND: YES
BUY ENTRY: NO

Do not output:

BUY 70%

merely because:

- Higher lows exist
- Momentum is positive
- Trend is bullish
- Volume recently increased

Those are supporting factors, not sufficient entry confirmation.

==================================================
12. NEW SIGNAL HIERARCHY
==================================================

Use this hierarchy:

A. BULLISH TREND
Price structure is bullish.

B. BULLISH SETUP
Price has a favorable setup.

C. BREAKOUT WATCH
Price is approaching resistance.

D. BREAKOUT CONFIRMED
Resistance has been broken with volume and price acceptance.

E. BUY
Breakout/entry conditions are confirmed with acceptable risk/reward.

F. BULL TRAP
Breakout failed and price returned below resistance.

G. BEARISH BREAKDOWN
Important support has failed.

==================================================
14. NEVER HIDE THE REASON FOR A SIGNAL
==================================================

Every signal must explain the primary reason.

For example:

BUY:
"Resistance broken with expanding volume and successful retest."

NO BUY:
"Bullish structure, but price is directly beneath resistance without breakout confirmation."

BULL TRAP:
"Price broke resistance but failed to hold above it; volume confirmation weakened."

BEARISH:
"Support failed with expanding selling volume."

==================================================
15. FINAL RULE
==================================================

THE SYSTEM MUST NEVER CONFUSE:

"THE MARKET IS BULLISH"

WITH:

"THIS IS A GOOD PRICE TO BUY."

Entry location matters.

Range position matters.

Resistance matters.

Volume confirmation matters.

Risk/reward matters.

Macro regime matters.

Breakout confirmation matters.

A bullish asset sitting directly underneath major resistance should NOT receive a high-confidence BUY until the resistance is actually broken and successfully held.

Preserve the existing UI and all existing features. Upgrade the underlying decision engine only.
