Upgrade my trading bot by implementing a rule-based candlestick price-action strategy inspired by the methodology taught in Ross Cameron / Warrior Trading’s video “Top 3 MOST Profitable Candlestick Chart Patterns.”

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT blindly trade whenever a candlestick pattern appears. The bot must evaluate the surrounding market context, trend, support/resistance, volume, momentum, and risk/reward before generating a signal.

The goal is to identify HIGH-CONVICTION LONG and SHORT setups and reject low-quality candle patterns.

==================================================

1. CANDLESTICK PATTERN ENGINE
    ==================================================

Detect these patterns:

BULLISH:

* Bullish engulfing
* Hammer / bullish rejection candle
* Morning star
* Strong bullish momentum candle
* Bullish reversal candle at support

BEARISH:

* Bearish engulfing
* Shooting star / bearish rejection candle
* Evening star
* Strong bearish momentum candle
* Bearish reversal candle at resistance

For every detected pattern calculate:

pattern_strength = 0-100

Do NOT consider a pattern tradable simply because it exists.

==================================================
2. MARKET CONTEXT

Before considering an entry, determine:

* Current trend
* Short-term trend
* Higher-high / higher-low structure
* Lower-high / lower-low structure
* Consolidation/range
* Breakout
* Breakdown
* Pullback
* Reversal
* Distance from VWAP
* Distance from major moving averages
* Relative volume
* Premarket high/low when applicable
* Previous day high/low
* Current day high/low
* Major support/resistance

Determine whether the stock is:

BULLISH
BEARISH
NEUTRAL
CHOPPY

Do not trade NEUTRAL or CHOPPY conditions unless a very strong breakout setup exists.

==================================================
3. SUPPORT & RESISTANCE

Automatically identify important price levels.

Prioritize:

1. Premarket high
2. Premarket low
3. Previous day high
4. Previous day low
5. Current day high
6. Current day low
7. VWAP
8. Recent swing highs
9. Recent swing lows
10. Major consolidation boundaries
11. High-volume price levels

Candlestick signals occurring directly at important support/resistance should receive significantly higher scores.

Example:

Bullish engulfing in the middle of nowhere:
LOW SCORE

Bullish engulfing after a pullback into major support:
HIGH SCORE

Bearish rejection candle at major resistance:
HIGH SCORE

==================================================
4. VOLUME CONFIRMATION

Calculate relative volume:

relative_volume =
current_volume / average_volume

Use:

< 0.75 = weak
0.75-1.0 = normal
1.0-1.5 = elevated
1.5-2.0 = strong

2.0 = very strong

Require increased volume for high-confidence breakout/reversal trades whenever possible.

A candle pattern occurring on extremely low volume should generally be rejected.

==================================================
5. VWAP FILTER

Use VWAP as an intraday trend filter.

LONG preference:

Price > VWAP
AND
VWAP is rising
AND
price holds/reclaims VWAP

SHORT preference:

Price < VWAP
AND
VWAP is falling
AND
price rejects/fails VWAP

Do not automatically reject trades crossing VWAP, but reduce confidence when VWAP and price action disagree.

==================================================
6. BULLISH SETUP

Generate a LONG candidate when several of the following occur:

* Bullish engulfing
* Hammer/rejection candle
* Morning star
* Strong bullish candle
* Breakout from consolidation
* Reclaim of VWAP
* Bounce from support
* Higher low
* Break above resistance
* Increasing volume
* Relative volume > 1.0
* Strong market/sector momentum

Highest-quality setup:

PULLBACK -> SUPPORT -> BULLISH REJECTION/ENGULFING -> VOLUME CONFIRMATION -> BREAK OF SIGNAL CANDLE HIGH

Entry:

Do NOT automatically enter at candle formation.

Wait for confirmation.

Preferred entry:

price > signal_candle_high

or

price breaks the relevant resistance level.

==================================================
7. BEARISH SETUP

Generate a SHORT candidate when several of the following occur:

* Bearish engulfing
* Shooting star/rejection candle
* Evening star
* Strong bearish candle
* Breakdown from consolidation
* Rejection at resistance
* Loss of VWAP
* Lower high
* Break below support
* Increasing selling volume
* Relative volume > 1.0
* Weak market/sector momentum

Highest-quality setup:

PUSH INTO RESISTANCE -> BEARISH REJECTION/ENGULFING -> VOLUME CONFIRMATION -> BREAK OF SIGNAL CANDLE LOW

Entry:

price < signal_candle_low

or

price breaks the relevant support level.

==================================================
8. BREAKOUT CONFIRMATION

Never buy a breakout simply because price touches resistance.

Require:

1. Price approaches resistance
2. Volume increases
3. Candle closes above resistance
4. Candle body demonstrates meaningful strength
5. Relative volume is elevated
6. Next candle confirms continuation OR breakout level successfully retests

Avoid:

* Low-volume breakouts
* Extremely extended candles
* Breakouts directly into major resistance
* Immediate failed breakouts
* Choppy price action

Detect potential FALSE BREAKOUTS.

If price breaks resistance and quickly falls back below it, label:

FAILED_BREAKOUT

and reduce long confidence substantially.

==================================================
9. BREAKDOWN CONFIRMATION

Mirror the breakout logic for shorts.

Require:

* Support violation
* Strong bearish candle
* Increased volume
* Close below support
* Confirmation or failed retest

Detect:

FAILED_BREAKDOWN

when price quickly reclaims support.

==================================================
10. SCORING SYSTEM

Create a 0-100 setup score.

LONG scoring:

+20 bullish candlestick pattern
+15 support/reclaim
+15 volume confirmation
+10 price above VWAP
+10 VWAP rising
+10 higher-low structure
+10 resistance breakout
+5 sector strength
+5 market strength

SHORT scoring:

+20 bearish candlestick pattern
+15 resistance/rejection
+15 volume confirmation
+10 price below VWAP
+10 VWAP falling
+10 lower-high structure
+10 support breakdown
+5 sector weakness
+5 market weakness

Maximum = 100.

Signal classification:

90-100 = VERY HIGH CONVICTION
80-89 = HIGH CONVICTION
70-79 = VALID SETUP
60-69 = WATCHLIST ONLY
<60 = NO TRADE

IMPORTANT:

The score is NOT a prediction of probability.

It is a setup-quality score.

==================================================
11. ENTRY RULES

Do not enter merely because score >= threshold.

Require:

setup_score >= 70

AND

confirmation trigger occurs

AND

risk/reward >= 2.0

AND

no major conflicting condition exists.

For VERY HIGH CONVICTION setups:

score >= 85

AND

volume confirmation

AND

price-action confirmation.

==================================================
12. STOP LOSS

For LONG:

Place stop below:

* signal candle low
    OR
* recent swing low
    OR
* support level

Use whichever provides logical market invalidation.

For SHORT:

Place stop above:

* signal candle high
    OR
* recent swing high
    OR
* resistance level

Never place a stop at an arbitrary fixed percentage if a technical invalidation level is available.

==================================================
13. PROFIT TARGETS

Calculate:

Risk = |entry - stop|

Target 1 = entry + 1R for LONG
Target 2 = entry + 2R
Target 3 = entry + 3R

For SHORT:

Target 1 = entry - 1R
Target 2 = entry - 2R
Target 3 = entry - 3R

Never enter if realistic resistance/support makes a 2R target impossible.

==================================================
14. TRADE MANAGEMENT

At +1R:

Consider taking partial profits.

Move stop toward breakeven only when market structure supports doing so.

At +2R:

Take another partial profit.

Allow remaining position to run toward +3R or trail using:

* higher lows for LONG
* lower highs for SHORT

Do NOT aggressively move stops simply because price moved slightly in favor.

==================================================
