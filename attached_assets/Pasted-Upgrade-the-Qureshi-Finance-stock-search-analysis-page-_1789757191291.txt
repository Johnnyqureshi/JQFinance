Upgrade the Qureshi Finance stock search/analysis page by integrating a real-time market heatmap/order-flow visualization that helps users understand potential price direction.

IMPORTANT:
Do not remove or break any existing scanner, stock analysis, game-plan, technical indicator, news, catalyst, or trading functionality. Add this as an additional analysis layer.

1. HEATMAP INTEGRATION

On the stock search/detail page, add a prominent section called:

“🔥 Liquidity & Direction Heatmap”

The heatmap should visualize available market liquidity/order-book pressure around the current price.

Display, when the connected market-data provider supports it:

* Current price
* Bid/ask levels
* Bid liquidity
* Ask liquidity
* Large liquidity walls
* Support liquidity zones
* Resistance liquidity zones
* Recent trades/order flow if available
* Volume concentration
* Price levels with unusually high liquidity
* Changes in liquidity over time

Use a dark, professional trading-terminal style.

The heatmap must update automatically using the fastest reliable data available.

2. DO NOT FAKE DATA

Never fabricate heatmap, order-book, liquidity, volume, or directional data.

If the current API/provider does not provide the required data, clearly display:

“Heatmap data unavailable from current market-data provider.”

Then show whatever legitimate data is available.

Do not substitute simulated numbers for real market data.

3. DIRECTION ENGINE

Create a “Heatmap Direction” analysis directly underneath the heatmap.

Analyze:

* Bid vs ask liquidity
* Liquidity imbalance
* Large bid walls
* Large ask walls
* Whether liquidity is moving closer to or farther from price
* Whether large walls are being absorbed
* Volume concentration
* Price movement relative to liquidity
* Recent aggressive buying/selling, if available
* Short-term momentum
* VWAP
* Relative volume
* RSI
* MACD
* Support/resistance
* ATR/volatility

Produce three possible states:

🟢 BULLISH PRESSURE
🔴 BEARISH PRESSURE
🟡 NEUTRAL / MIXED

IMPORTANT:
The heatmap should NOT independently determine direction.

Combine heatmap information with the existing technical-analysis engine.

4. HEATMAP + TECHNICAL CONFLUENCE

Create a confluence calculation.

Example:

Heatmap:
Bullish

Relative Volume:
Bullish

Price vs VWAP:
Bullish

RSI:
Neutral

Momentum:
Bullish

Overall:
“BULLISH PRESSURE — 4/5 signals aligned”

Do NOT present this as a guaranteed prediction.

Use wording such as:

“Current market structure suggests bullish pressure.”

“Liquidity currently favors the upside.”

“Signals are mixed; directional conviction is low.”

“Large resistance liquidity is currently limiting upside.”

5. LIQUIDITY WALL DETECTION

Automatically identify unusually large liquidity concentrations.

For example:

“Large bid wall detected near $80,500”

“Large ask wall detected near $81,200”

Then explain what that could mean:

“Bid liquidity may provide short-term support if it remains in place.”

“Ask liquidity may act as near-term resistance if sellers continue defending the level.”

IMPORTANT:
Do not assume a wall will hold.

Detect whether the wall is:

* Newly appearing
* Increasing
* Decreasing
* Being consumed
* Moving
* Cancelled

This distinction is critical because liquidity can disappear.

6. ABSORPTION DETECTION

Add detection for possible absorption.

Examples:

Heavy buying + price fails to move higher
→ possible seller absorption

Heavy selling + price refuses to move lower
→ possible buyer absorption

Display:

“⚠️ Possible seller absorption near resistance”

or

“🟢 Possible buyer absorption near support”

Only display this when supported by actual available data.

7. LIQUIDITY SHIFT

Track changes in the heatmap.

For example:

Previous:
Bid liquidity $8.2M
Ask liquidity $11.5M

Current:
Bid liquidity $12.4M
Ask liquidity $9.1M

Then show:

“Liquidity shifted toward buyers.”

This should update dynamically.

8. DIRECTION HISTORY

Add a small timeline showing how the directional state has changed.

Example:

10:30 — 🟢 Bullish
10:35 — 🟢 Bullish
10:40 — 🟡 Neutral
10:45 — 🔴 Bearish
10:50 — 🔴 Bearish

This allows users to see whether bullish/bearish pressure is strengthening or weakening instead of relying on one snapshot.

9. HEATMAP WARNING SYSTEM

Add warnings for potentially misleading conditions.

Examples:

“⚠️ Large liquidity wall detected but price is approaching it rapidly.”

“⚠️ Liquidity is disappearing — directional signal reliability reduced.”

“⚠️ Heavy buying detected without price expansion — possible absorption.”

“⚠️ Low volume — heatmap signal may be unreliable.”

“⚠️ Conflicting technical and liquidity signals.”

Use:

* WebSockets where available
* Efficient state updates
* Debouncing/throttling
* Incremental rendering
* Cached historical heatmap data where appropriate

Do not repeatedly reload the entire page.

14. MOBILE RESPONSIVENESS

The heatmap must work on desktop and mobile.

On mobile:

* Allow horizontal scrolling/zooming where necessary
* Keep current price visible
* Keep direction status visible
* Keep support/resistance levels readable
* Do not make the heatmap so large that users cannot access the Game Plan.

15. DIRECTION SCORE

Create a transparent directional score from the available signals.

Example:

Heatmap: +2
Liquidity imbalance: +2
Relative volume: +1
VWAP: +1
Momentum: +1
RSI: 0

Total: +7

Then map it to:

+5 to +10 = Bullish pressure
-4 to +4 = Neutral/mixed
-5 to -10 = Bearish pressure

BUT:
Do not hide the individual components.

Users must be able to see WHY the system reached the directional assessment.

Do not claim the score predicts the future price.

16. IMPORTANT MARKET MICROSTRUCTURE RULE

The system must understand that an order-book wall is NOT automatically support or resistance.

Liquidity can:

* Move
* Cancel
* Be spoofed
* Be absorbed
* Be replenished
* Be consumed

Therefore, weight actual executed volume/trades and price reaction more heavily than simply seeing a large resting order.
